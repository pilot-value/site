About Eurowings

Eurowings is the Lufthansa Group’s point-to-point carrier, headquartered in Düsseldorf. It flies a single-family Airbus A320 fleet on short- and medium-haul routes from bases across Germany and Europe, and operates no long-haul network. Fares are low-cost, but cockpit pay is not individually negotiated: it is set by a collective agreement pay scale with the German pilots’ union Vereinigung Cockpit (VC), so a pilot’s annual figure follows their step and seniority. First officers may apply without a type rating — Eurowings covers the full A320-family type rating, which is what separates it from pay-to-fly low-cost operators.

Routes & Fleet

Network

Eurowings serves more than 140 destinations across Europe and the Mediterranean from German bases including Düsseldorf, Cologne/Bonn, Hamburg and Stuttgart. It is a short- and medium-haul point-to-point network, not a connecting hub operation.

Fleet

Airbus A319 / A320 / A320neo / A321. A single-family fleet, so pilots can stay on one type for their whole career here.

Training

Training & Checkrides

Type rating (company-funded)

LIFUS

Line training under a training captain after the type rating. High-frequency short-haul flying builds sectors quickly.

EASA-standard proficiency and line checks, once or twice a year.

Upgrade to captain

A Fast Track Upgrade to Captain (A320) is advertised for serving first officers.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Eurowings captain salary in 2026?

Eurowings captains average about ¥27.7M a year (range ¥20.6M–¥34.8M), before tax. Pay is set by the Vereinigung Cockpit collective agreement pay scale, which runs to 23 steps for captains, so where a pilot sits in that range depends on their step rather than on individual negotiation.

What is Eurowings first officer salary?

Eurowings first officers average about ¥17.1M a year (range ¥12.7M–¥21.5M), before tax. The first officer scale has 14 steps, and pay moves up with time in seat until upgrade to captain.

Does Eurowings pay for the type rating?

Yes. Eurowings recruits first officers who do not hold a type rating and covers the full Airbus A320-family type rating. That is the main difference from low-cost operators that ask pilots to fund their own type rating.

How does Eurowings pay compare with ANA and JAL?

Eurowings captains average ¥27.7M against ¥27M at ANA and JAL, and first officers ¥17.1M against ¥18M. All four figures are gross, before income tax; both Germany and Japan tax pilot income.

What is Eurowings pilot salary per month?

Dividing the annual average by 12, Eurowings captains earn about ¥2.31M a month and first officers about ¥1.43M. What actually lands each month moves with block hours flown, and where an annual bonus is paid the regular monthly figure sits lower with a spike in the bonus month.

What is the Eurowings pilot pay scale?

Eurowings first officers average ¥17.1M and captains ¥27.7M — a step of about ¥10.6M, or roughly 1.6×. The full range runs ¥12.7M to ¥34.8M, and where you sit inside it is set by seniority, type rating and the routes you fly. The seniority table on this page breaks the same range into career stages.

Is Eurowings pilot salary before or after tax?

Every figure on this page is gross, before income tax. Captains average ¥27.7M and first officers ¥17.1M pre-tax, and income tax, social insurance and any local levies come out of that. Take-home depends on where you are resident and on your own deductions, so no single percentage applies. Airlines based in countries with no personal income tax — the UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia — leave more of the same gross figure in your hand.
