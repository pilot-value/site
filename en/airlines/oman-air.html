About Oman Air

Oman Air is Oman's state-owned flag carrier, operating from its Muscat hub to Asia, Europe, Africa and across the Middle East. Oman has had no personal income tax, but Royal Decree 56/2025 introduces a 5% personal income tax from January 2028 on taxable income above OMR 42,000 a year (tax-free until 2027). The carrier recruits foreign pilots and backs them with generous housing, medical and education support. It joined oneworld on 30 June 2025.

Its fleet is built around the Boeing 787 and Boeing 737. While the network is relatively compact, Oman Air is well regarded for its high-quality service.

Training

Training & Checkrides

Type Rating (EASA/GCAA/GACA approved)

Type rating earned at an EASA-approved ATO or with a Gulf civil aviation authority (UAE, Saudi, Qatar). Ground school → simulator → LIFUS.

LIFUS (Line Training)

After the type rating, Line Flying Under Supervision with an instructor captain — typically 50–80 legs.

Recurrent Checks (OPC/LPC)

Proficiency checks once or twice a year, supervised by national authorities to ICAO standards.

Command Upgrade

Seniority- or merit-based. Gulf full-service carriers weigh flight hours and internal assessments, and have a strong track record of hiring foreign captains.

Tax-free until 2027. Oman has had no personal income tax, but Royal Decree 56/2025 introduces a 5% personal income tax from January 2028 on taxable income above OMR 42,000 a year. From 2028 gross pay is no longer the same as take-home. Your own residency and tax position needs separate advice.
