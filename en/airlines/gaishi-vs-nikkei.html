How to Choose

Foreign vs Japanese — the real advantages of each

Reasons to go foreign

💰 Take-home pay roughly doubles
Between the UAE's tax-free regime and US pay levels, the same job leaves you with around twice what you keep at ANA.
⚡ Faster upgrade to Captain
Emirates and Qatar Airways upgrade First Officers to Captain within five to eight years of joining — far faster than the 15 to 18 years at ANA.
🌍 A route network on the world's largest scale
More than 150 cities, every route international. Your experience and perspective as a pilot broaden enormously.
✈️ Get onto the newest aircraft sooner
Emirates operates the world's largest A380 fleet. There are opportunities to fly widebodies relatively early after joining.

Reasons to stay with a Japanese carrier (ANA / JAL)

👨‍👩‍👧 You can live in Japan with your family and relatives
No unaccompanied overseas posting, none of the strain of moving the family abroad. The reassurance of being on hand for your children's schooling and your parents' care.
🛡 Job security
During the 2020 pandemic Emirates made large-scale redundancies. The Japanese majors kept protecting jobs throughout.
🏛 Pensions and retirement benefits
The employees' pension, the corporate pension and a lump-sum retirement scheme are all in place, making life after retirement far easier to plan.
🔄 You work in Japanese
No language barrier. You can keep flying for the long haul, without stress, inside a familiar culture and way of working.

The verdict: there is no right answer — decide on your own priorities in life

If maximizing salary and take-home pay is the goal, go foreign. If family, stability and retirement come first, stay with a Japanese carrier. Which one is right depends on your own values and your family's circumstances. A move to a foreign carrier tends to be an irreversible decision — returning to ANA or JAL is difficult — so weigh it carefully against your total life plan.
