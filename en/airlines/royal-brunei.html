FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Royal Brunei Airlines captain salary in 2026?

Royal Brunei Airlines captain pay is estimated at about $129K per year, 100% tax-free (no personal income tax). These are our own estimates from public information, not figures the airline publishes.

What is the Royal Brunei Airlines first officer salary?

Royal Brunei Airlines first officer pay is estimated at about $69K per year, 100% tax-free (no personal income tax), rising with seniority.

Is Royal Brunei Airlines pilot pay really tax-free?

Yes — there is no personal income tax in the home country, so pilots keep 100% of gross pay.

What are the requirements to become a Royal Brunei Airlines pilot?

Captain — B787-8 — Foreign direct entry (tax-free). Key requirements: License: ATPL (ICAO); Type: B787 type preferred; Contract: USD (tax-free). We have not been able to confirm flight-hour requirements the airline publishes; the airline’s careers page is linked under Jobs above.

What is the Royal Brunei Airlines pilot salary per month?

Dividing our estimated annual figures by 12, Royal Brunei Airlines captains earn about $11K a month and first officers about $5.8K. What actually lands each month moves with block hours flown, and where an annual bonus is paid the regular monthly figure sits lower with a spike in the bonus month.

What is the Royal Brunei Airlines pilot pay scale?

We estimate Royal Brunei Airlines first officers at $69K and captains at $129K — a step of about $60K, or roughly 1.9×. The actual figure moves with seniority, type rating and the routes you fly.

Salary figures are our own estimates. Verify actual conditions on each airline's official website.
