Frequently asked questions

How much do pilots earn in Latin America?
Across the 4 Latin America airlines on PILOT VALUE, captains average ¥2,050万 (¥1,200万〜3,000万) and first officers average ¥1,050万 (¥550万〜1,700万). That is about 17% below the ¥2,480万 captain average across all 112 airlines we track, and 6th of the 7 regions we group them into.
Which airline in Latin America pays pilots the most?
Of the 4 airlines listed, Aeromexico pays the most: captains average ¥2,300万 (¥1,700万〜2,900万) and first officers ¥1,200万 (¥850万〜1,700万).
Which country in Latin America pays pilots the most?
Of the 4 countries in Latin America that we cover, Mexico is highest: captains at its 1 listed airline average ¥2,300万.
What is the monthly pilot salary in Latin America?
Dividing the regional average by 12 gives roughly ¥171万 a month for captains and ¥88万 for first officers. What actually lands each month moves with block hours flown, and where an annual bonus is paid the regular monthly figure sits lower with a spike in the bonus month.
How does it compare with Japanese airlines?
Captains at the 26 Japanese airlines we list average ¥1,850万. Latin America at ¥2,050万 is ¥200万 higher.
