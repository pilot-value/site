Frequently Asked Questions

Where does the annual salary data on this site come from?
The figures are reference values compiled from each airline's public disclosures, industry reports, recruitment pages, and information from pilot communities. Actual pay varies with years of experience, aircraft type, and individual contract terms. Please always confirm the details with each airline's official information.
Do I need to create an account?
No account is required to view airline salaries and information. A free account is required to join the community and post.
How do I switch between dark and light mode?
Click the moon/sun icon button in the navigation at the top right of the screen to switch. Your setting is saved in your browser.
What should I do if information is out of date or incorrect?
Please let us know the page in question and the correction via the contact form on the community page. We will verify it and respond promptly.
Can I use the site on a smartphone?
Yes. This site is designed mobile-first, so it works comfortably on smartphones and tablets.
What if I forget my password?
Use "Forgot your password?" on the login page and we will send a 6-digit code to your registered email address. Once you sign in with the code, you can set a new password right there. If you signed up with Google, you can add a password the same way.
How do I close my account?
You can request account closure from the "Close account" button at the bottom of the profile page. Your data will be deleted within 30 days of closure.
The map on the World Airlines page does not work
Please check that JavaScript is enabled. Try clearing your browser cache and reloading the page. If the problem persists, please contact us.

Still need help?

If the above does not resolve your issue, please contact us via the contact form on the community page. We will respond as quickly as we can.
