Frequently asked questions

How much do pilots earn in the USA?
Across the 11 airlines we list in the USA, captains average ¥4,380万 (¥1,850万〜8,690万) and first officers average ¥2,520万 (¥900万〜5,260万). That is about 77% above the ¥2,480万 captain average across all 112 airlines we track.
Which airline in the USA pays pilots the most?
Of the 11 airlines listed, United Airlines pays the most: captains average ¥6,320万 (¥5,370万〜8,530万) and first officers ¥3,870万.
How does the USA rank worldwide for pilot pay?
Ranked by captain average across the 51 countries we cover, the USA is number 1. The world average is ¥2,480万; the USA sits at ¥4,380万.
How does it compare with Japanese airlines?
