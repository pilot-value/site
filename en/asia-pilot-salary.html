Why we do not rank Asia

We are working through the Asia airlines one at a time to trace where each figure came from. Doing that showed that numbers sitting side by side under the word “salary” are not the same kind of number: cash an airline published in a vacancy, a package value that folds in housing and school fees, a ceiling quoted in an agency advert, a figure written by a pilot who works there. Averaging those together cannot answer which airline pays more.

So this page publishes no regional average, no ranking between regions and no multiple against the world average. Instead the tables above give each airline's figure together with who it applies to and on what terms. An unweighted mean of company figures is not the average pay of pilots in a region.

Frequently asked questions

How much do pilots earn in Asia?
We do not publish a regional average for the 26 airlines we list in Asia. The figures come from different kinds of source — the airline's own vacancy, a recruitment agency advert, a pilot's own report — and some are cash pay while others include housing and school fees. They are not on one scale, so averaging them produces a number that cannot be compared. We do publish figures for 4: EVA Air — First officer (four-year contract): $100K (annual pay (the figure the airline says is exceeded), Official posting). Cathay Pacific — 副操縦士（First Officer）: $123K (target annual salary, Modelled example); 二等航空士（Second Officer）: $80K (target annual salary, Modelled example). STARLUX Airlines — 昇格後（機長歴10年目安）: $173K (monthly pay × 13 months, Modelled example).
Which airline in Asia pays pilots the most?
We do not rank them. The figures come from different kinds of source — the airline's own vacancy, a recruitment agency advert, a pilot's own report — and some are cash pay while others include housing and school fees. They are not on one scale, so averaging them produces a number that cannot be compared. Each airline's page shows what its figure covers, who it applies to and when it was published.
Which country in Asia pays pilots the most?
We do not rank the countries either. A country figure is the mean of its airlines' figures, and in Asia those figures do not mean the same thing, so we are not publishing that mean.
What is the monthly pilot salary in Asia?
We do not publish a monthly figure, because we do not publish the regional annual average it would be divided from. Where an airline publishes a monthly figure itself, it is on that airline's page. An annual figure divided by twelve is not what lands each month.
Is pilot pay in Asia tax-free?
1 of the 26 airlines listed is based in a country that levies no personal income tax, so for that one the figures we publish are close to take-home.
