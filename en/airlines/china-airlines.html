Overview

About China Airlines

China Airlines (CAL) is Taiwan's flag carrier. Hubbing at Taoyuan Int'l Airport, it serves Americas, Europe, Asia, and Oceania. Note: despite the name, this is the carrier of Taiwan (Republic of China), not Mainland China (PRC). A major freighter operator as well.

Comparison

China Airlines vs the other Taiwanese carriers

On our estimates a China Airlines captain is on $126K a year against $186K at EVA Air and $195K at STARLUX, and a China Airlines first officer on $69K against $100K and $123K. All six are our own estimates, gross. Income tax applies in Taiwan, so none of these is a tax-free package. A side-by-side of the three is on the Taiwan comparison page.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is China Airlines captain salary in 2026?

Our estimate for a China Airlines captain is $126K a year, gross, before income tax. It is our own estimate from public information; the actual figure moves with the aircraft type, years of service and the hours you fly.

What is China Airlines first officer salary?

Our estimate for a China Airlines first officer is $69K a year, gross. The English posting gives the requirements — 1,500 total flight hours and 500 hours on type. The actual figure moves with the aircraft type, years of service and the hours you fly.

What are the requirements to become a China Airlines pilot?

On the English posting a captain needs 5,000 total flight hours, 1,000 hours in command and 500 hours on type; a first officer needs 1,500 total and 500 on type, with an ICAO ATPL. The Chinese version of the page asks for different hours (3,500 total, 800 in command) and requires Taiwanese nationality in every category.

What is China Airlines pilot salary per month?

We do not publish a monthly figure for China Airlines. An annual figure divided by twelve is not what lands in a given month — what you are paid moves with the hours you fly.

What is the China Airlines pilot pay scale?

We estimate annual pay for China Airlines captains at about $126K, with a range of roughly $94K–$154K. That is our own estimate from public information, not a figure China Airlines publishes. These are gross figures, before income tax. Where you sit inside the range is set by the aircraft type, years of service and the routes you fly. We estimate annual pay for China Airlines first officers at about $69K, with a range of roughly $44K–$81K. That is our own estimate from public information, not a figure China Airlines publishes. These are gross figures, before income tax. Where you sit inside the range is set by the aircraft type, years of service and the routes you fly.

Is China Airlines pilot salary before or after tax?

Every figure on this page is gross, before income tax. Take-home depends on where you are resident and on your own deductions, so no single percentage applies, and we do not publish one. Airlines based in countries with no personal income tax — the UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia — leave more of the same gross figure in your hand.
