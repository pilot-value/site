FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is China Southern Airlines captain salary in 2026?

China Southern Airlines captain pay averages about ¥29.5M per year (range ¥21M–¥47M), pre-tax. Figures are reference values based on public data and industry benchmarks.

What is China Southern Airlines first officer salary?

China Southern Airlines first officer pay averages about ¥15M per year (range ¥9.8M–¥21M), pre-tax, rising with seniority — see the Career Ladder above for the year-by-year breakdown.

What are the requirements to become a China Southern Airlines pilot?

Contract Captain (Foreign) — International (irregular). Key requirements — License: ATPL (ICAO); CAAC: License conversion required.

How does China Southern Airlines pay compare to ANA or JAL?

China Southern Airlines captains average ¥29.5M, versus roughly ¥27M at ANA / JAL. Both figures are pre-tax; Japan applies ~33% income tax.

What is China Southern Airlines pilot salary per month?

Dividing the annual average by 12, China Southern Airlines captains earn about ¥2.46M a month and first officers about ¥1.25M. What actually lands each month moves with block hours flown, and where an annual bonus is paid the regular monthly figure sits lower with a spike in the bonus month.

What is the China Southern Airlines pilot pay scale?

China Southern Airlines first officers average ¥15M and captains ¥29.5M — a step of about ¥14.5M, or roughly 2.0×. The full range runs ¥9.8M to ¥47M, and where you sit inside it is set by seniority, type rating and the routes you fly. The seniority table on this page breaks the same range into career stages.

Is China Southern Airlines pilot salary before or after tax?

Every figure on this page is gross, before income tax. Captains average ¥29.5M and first officers ¥15M pre-tax, and income tax, social insurance and any local levies come out of that. Take-home depends on where you are resident and on your own deductions, so no single percentage applies. Airlines based in countries with no personal income tax — the UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia — leave more of the same gross figure in your hand.
