How to choose

ANA vs JAL — which one should you choose?

ANA

ANA suits you if…

  • ✦The Star Alliance route network (Lufthansa, United and the rest) appeals to you
  • ✦You want to build experience on the broader international network (90+ countries)
  • ✦You want time on an unusual type — the A380 on the Honolulu route
  • ✦A bold, innovation-driven corporate culture speaks to you
  • ✦You want broad fleet exposure, from the domestic A320 to the international B777
  • ✦You want the earliest possible upgrade to captain
JAL

JAL suits you if…

  • ✦The Oneworld route network (British Airways, American, Qatar Airways and the rest) appeals to you
  • ✦You want to fly a state-of-the-art type like the A350 (first introduced in Japan by JAL)
  • ✦You like the odds that come with a bigger intake (around 50 a year)
  • ✦You value the special bonus paid on completing five years as captain (460万円)
  • ✦You identify with a long-established, tradition-minded culture and history
  • ✦You are coming to flying after a first career (career-changer route)

Verdict: on pay alone it makes no difference — in the end, decide on which airline you actually want to fly for

Both ANA and JAL sit at Japan's ceiling, an average of 2,700万円 for captains, and take-home and benefits are all but identical too. What matters when you go into selection is being able to give the interview panel a clear reason — "because I want to be at ANA", "because I love JAL's fleet". Both companies are looking for people who are genuinely serious about becoming pilots.

FAQ

ANA vs JAL: frequently asked questions

Which pays more for pilots, ANA or JAL?+
Captains and first officers are on effectively the same level at both (captains average 2,700万円, first officers 1,800万円). JAL does have a scheme of its own: a 460万円 bonus on completing five years after upgrading to captain.
Which is easier to get into, ANA's or JAL's company-sponsored cadet program?+
JAL takes around 50 cadets a year, a comparatively large intake, while ANA tends to take fewer — in the region of 10 to 30. JAL also draws more applicants, though, so competition at both runs into the hundreds to one. Assume there is no great difference in your chances.
Which airline gets you to captain faster, ANA or JAL?+
ANA tends to be marginally quicker — figure on 15 to 18 years at ANA versus 15 to 20 at JAL. It swings widely, however, with individual assessments, medical status and each carrier's fleet requirements. Neither company offers any guarantee that you will make captain early.
Do ANA and JAL run direct-entry hiring for licensed line pilots?+
As of 2026, neither ANA nor JAL advertises direct-entry recruitment for serving pilots (CPL/ATPL holders). At both carriers the main ways in are the company-sponsored cadet program or junior license-holder recruitment. Line pilots weighing a move to a foreign or low-cost carrier are better advised to look at foreign carriers such as Emirates and Singapore Airlines.
Whose staff travel discounts are easier to use, ANA's or JAL's?+
Both run discounted-ticket schemes for staff and their families. ANA employees also have ready access to discounts across Star Alliance carriers (Lufthansa, United, Thai Airways and others), while JAL employees can use discounts across Oneworld (British Airways, American and others). Choosing on where you like to travel is one perfectly good approach.
