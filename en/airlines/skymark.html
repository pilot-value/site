About Skymark Airlines

Skymark Airlines is an independent carrier founded on 12 November 1996 (flying from 1998), ranking third in Japan behind ANA and JAL. Operating domestic routes from Haneda to major cities nationwide.

After filing for civil rehabilitation in 2015, Skymark successfully restructured and relisted on the Tokyo Stock Exchange on 14 December 2022. With competitive pricing and efficient operations, the airline continues stable growth. Domestic-only operations mean short-haul, high-frequency flying style.

Whose pay, what it includes, and when it is from

Pilot pay — the only published average covers flight crew as a whole

Skymark publishes the average annual pay for its 290 flight crew — captains and first officers together — in its statutory annual report. It publishes no average for either rank on its own. The vacancy notices for experienced hires (B737 captain and first officer candidates) state no figure: the airline says pay is not disclosed and follows internal rules.
The averages and ranges by rank shown here previously had no record of which document they came from. Until a source can be verified the published figure by rank reads "Under review". The original values are kept in our records.

What is Skymark Airlines captain salary in 2026?

Skymark does not publish an average for captains on their own. What it does publish is the average for flight crew as a whole — captains and first officers together, 290 people — at $104K a year as at 31 March 2025, including bonuses and non-standard wages, for Skymark alone. That is not a captain average. For experienced hires (B737 captain candidates) the airline states that pay is not disclosed and follows internal rules. What are the requirements to become a Skymark Airlines pilot?

The airline states that pay is not disclosed and follows internal rules. For its company-trained cadet pilot programme the published requirements include TOEIC 750 or IELTS Overall 6.0 and corrected vision of 0.7 or better (1.0 or better in both eyes), and anyone who holds a commercial pilot licence cannot apply. How does Skymark Airlines pay compare to ANA or JAL?

There is no pair of figures on the same basis to compare. Neither is an average for captains or for first officers, and the populations, scope and dates differ, so we do not say which airline pays more.
