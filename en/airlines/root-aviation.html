FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Root Aviation captain salary in 2026?

Root Aviation captain pay averages about ¥13M per year (range ¥10M–¥18M), pre-tax. Figures are reference values based on public data and industry benchmarks.

What is Root Aviation first officer salary?

Root Aviation first officer pay averages about ¥7.2M per year (range ¥5M–¥10M), pre-tax, rising with seniority — see the Career Ladder above for the year-by-year breakdown.

What are the requirements to become a Root Aviation pilot?

B777 PIC Upgrade Position — For pilots with B747 experience. Key requirements — Eligibility: Captains with B747-400 / B747-800 experience; Position: Upgrade to B777 Captain (PIC); Region: Asia (details undisclosed). For details, apply via Latest Pilot Jobs or contact Root Aviation directly.

How does Root Aviation pay compare to ANA or JAL?

Root Aviation captains average ¥13M, versus roughly ¥27M at ANA / JAL. Both figures are pre-tax; Japan applies ~33% income tax.

What is Root Aviation pilot salary per month?

Dividing the annual average by 12, Root Aviation captains earn about ¥1.08M a month and first officers about ¥0.6M. What actually lands each month moves with block hours flown, and where an annual bonus is paid the regular monthly figure sits lower with a spike in the bonus month.

What is the Root Aviation pilot pay scale?

Root Aviation first officers average ¥7.2M and captains ¥13M — a step of about ¥5.8M, or roughly 1.8×. The full range runs ¥5M to ¥18M, and where you sit inside it is set by seniority, type rating and the routes you fly. The seniority table on this page breaks the same range into career stages.

Is Root Aviation pilot salary before or after tax?

Every figure on this page is gross, before income tax. Captains average ¥13M and first officers ¥7.2M pre-tax, and income tax, social insurance and any local levies come out of that. Take-home depends on where you are resident and on your own deductions, so no single percentage applies. Airlines based in countries with no personal income tax — the UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia — leave more of the same gross figure in your hand.
