FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Contour Airlines captain salary in 2026?

We estimate Contour Airlines captain pay at about $156K a year: the year-10 captain salary of $147,365.83 at the top of Contour’s 2024 pay scale, plus the 6% 401(k) match.

What is Contour Airlines first officer salary?

We estimate Contour Airlines first officer pay at about $43K a year: the year-6 first officer salary of $40,730.70 on the same scale, plus the 6% match.

What is the Contour Airlines pilot salary per month?

We do not publish a monthly figure for Contour Airlines. An annual figure divided by twelve is not what lands in a given month — what you are paid moves with the hours you fly.

What is the Contour Airlines pilot pay scale?

We estimate annual pay for Contour Airlines captains at about $156K. That is our own estimate from public information, not a figure Contour Airlines publishes. These are gross figures, before income tax. The actual figure moves with the aircraft type, years of service and the routes you fly. Is the Contour Airlines pilot salary before or after tax?

Every figure on this page is gross, before income tax. Take-home depends on where you are resident and on your own deductions, so no single percentage applies, and we do not publish one. Airlines based in countries with no personal income tax — the UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia — leave more of the same gross figure in your hand.
