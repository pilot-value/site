Frequently asked questions

How much do pilots earn in Europe?
Across the 25 Europe airlines on PILOT VALUE, captains average ¥2,560万 (¥1,030万〜6,020万) and first officers average ¥1,390万 (¥480万〜4,370万). That is about 3% above the ¥2,480万 captain average across all 112 airlines we track, and 4th of the 7 regions we group them into.
Which airline in Europe pays pilots the most?
Of the 25 airlines listed, Swiss International Air Lines (SWISS) pays the most: captains average ¥4,100万 (¥3,520万〜5,900万) and first officers ¥2,500万 (¥1,850万〜3,400万).
Which country in Europe pays pilots the most?
Of the 18 countries in Europe that we cover, Switzerland is highest: captains at its 1 listed airline average ¥4,100万.
What is the monthly pilot salary in Europe?
Dividing the regional average by 12 gives roughly ¥213万 a month for captains and ¥116万 for first officers. What actually lands each month moves with block hours flown, and where an annual bonus is paid the regular monthly figure sits lower with a spike in the bonus month.
How does it compare with Japanese airlines?
Captains at the 26 Japanese airlines we list average ¥1,850万. Europe at ¥2,560万 is ¥710万 higher.
