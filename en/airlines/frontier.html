About Frontier Airlines

Frontier Airlines is a Denver-based ULCC known for its distinctive branding, which gives every aircraft a different animal design on its tail. With a fleet standardized on the A320 family, it serves destinations across the United States, the Caribbean, Mexico, and Latin America. Merger talks with Spirit Airlines broke down in 2023. Operating highly fuel-efficient aircraft, the carrier positions itself as a "green airline".

Operations

Routes & Fleet

Routes

Flies from the Denver hub to destinations throughout the United States, the Caribbean, Mexico, and Latin America. A large share of the network is seasonal.

Fleet

Airbus A321neo, A320neo, A319. Approximately 130 aircraft.

Training

Training & Checkrides

Type Rating Training (FAA-approved)

Type rating training at an FAA-certified ATO (Approved Training Organization), using Part 142 training centers such as CAE and FlightSafety. Ground school → simulator → LOFT.

IOE (Initial Operating Experience)

After the type rating is issued, Initial Operating Experience is flown under the supervision of an instructor captain (Check Airman) — typically around 25–50 legs.

Recurrent Checks (PC/LOE)

A Proficiency Check (PC) or Line Operational Evaluation (LOE) once or twice a year, conducted in accordance with FAA Part 121/135.

Upgrade to Captain

Seniority-based as a rule. Candidates must meet the required flight hours (normally 5,000–8,000 hours or more) and pass a check ride with a Check Airman. An R-ATP (1,500h) provision is available.

Benefits

Pilot Benefits & Allowances

✈️
Staff Travel (Pass)

Pass travel for the pilot and family — free or heavily discounted travel on Frontier's own flights and those of partner carriers.

🏥
Medical, Dental & Vision Insurance

Comprehensive health insurance for the pilot and family. Loss-of-license insurance is also standard.

💰
401(k) Retirement Plan

A defined-contribution 401(k) plan with company matching; matches of up to 5–16% are common.

📅
Paid Leave

Roughly 15–30 days a year, increasing with seniority. Flexible leave options such as flip and skip are available.

💵
Per Diem

A daily allowance on flight days (roughly $2–4 per hour). Rates differ between domestic and international flying.

🌐
International Flying Allowance

Additional allowances and hotel expenses for crew operating international flights.

What is Frontier Airlines captain salary in 2026?

What is Frontier Airlines first officer salary?

How does Frontier Airlines pay compare to ANA or JAL?

Frontier Airlines captains average ¥33M, versus roughly ¥27M at ANA / JAL. Both figures are pre-tax; Japan applies ~33% income tax.

What is Frontier Airlines pilot salary per month?

Dividing the annual average by 12, Frontier Airlines captains earn about ¥2.75M a month and first officers about ¥1.46M. What actually lands each month moves with block hours flown, and where an annual bonus is paid the regular monthly figure sits lower with a spike in the bonus month.

What is the Frontier Airlines pilot pay scale?

Frontier Airlines first officers average ¥17.5M and captains ¥33M — a step of about ¥15.5M, or roughly 1.9×. The full range runs ¥11M to ¥43M, and where you sit inside it is set by seniority, type rating and the routes you fly. The seniority table on this page breaks the same range into career stages.

Is Frontier Airlines pilot salary before or after tax?

Every figure on this page is gross, before income tax. Captains average ¥33M and first officers ¥17.5M pre-tax, and income tax, social insurance and any local levies come out of that. Take-home depends on where you are resident and on your own deductions, so no single percentage applies. Airlines based in countries with no personal income tax — the UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia — leave more of the same gross figure in your hand.
