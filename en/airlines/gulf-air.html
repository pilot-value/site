About Gulf Air

Founded in 1950, Gulf Air is one of the oldest airlines in the Middle East and the national flag carrier of Bahrain, serving Asia, Europe, Africa and the wider Middle East from its Manama hub.

Bahrain levies no personal income tax, and Gulf Air has a long track record of hiring foreign pilots on generous, family-friendly packages. It runs a mixed fleet of A320/A321 and A330 aircraft.

Routes & Fleet

Routes

From the Bahrain hub to Europe (London, Athens), Asia (Thailand, Sri Lanka, India), Africa and across the Middle East — 50+ destinations.

Fleet

Airbus A321neo, A321ceo and A330-200/300 — about 30 aircraft.

Training

Training & Checkrides

Type Rating (EASA/GCAA/GACA approved)

Type rating obtained at a Gulf/EASA-approved ATO (UAE, Saudi, Qatar authorities). Ground school → simulator → LIFUS.

LIFUS (Line Training)

After the type rating, Line Flying Under Supervision with an instructor captain, typically around 50–80 legs.

Recurrent Checks (OPC/LPC)

Proficiency checks 1–2 times a year, supervised by the relevant national authority to ICAO standards.

Command Upgrade

Seniority- or merit-based. Gulf full-service carriers weight flight hours and internal assessment; many foreign captains are hired.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Gulf Air captain salary in 2026?

Gulf Air captain pay averages about ¥27M per year (range ¥23M–¥33M), 100% tax-free (no personal income tax). Figures are reference values based on public data and industry benchmarks.

What is Gulf Air first officer salary?

Gulf Air first officer pay averages about ¥15M per year (range ¥12.5M–¥18.5M), 100% tax-free (no personal income tax), rising with seniority — see the Career Ladder above for the year-by-year breakdown.

Is Gulf Air salary really tax-free?

Yes — there is no personal income tax in the home country, so pilots keep 100% of gross pay. For a Japanese pilot that is roughly 1.5× the take-home of an equivalent ANA / JAL salary, which is taxed around 33%.

How does Gulf Air pay compare to ANA or JAL?

Gulf Air captains average ¥27M tax-free, versus roughly ¥27M pre-tax at ANA / JAL (taxed ~33%). After tax the overseas gap is even wider.

What is Gulf Air pilot salary per month?

Dividing the annual average by 12, Gulf Air captains earn about ¥2.25M a month and first officers about ¥1.25M. What actually lands each month moves with block hours flown, and where an annual bonus is paid the regular monthly figure sits lower with a spike in the bonus month.

What is the Gulf Air pilot pay scale?

Gulf Air first officers average ¥15M and captains ¥27M — a step of about ¥12M, or roughly 1.8×. The full range runs ¥12.5M to ¥33M, and where you sit inside it is set by seniority, type rating and the routes you fly. The seniority table on this page breaks the same range into career stages.
