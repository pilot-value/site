About Garuda Indonesia

Garuda Indonesia, founded 1949, is Indonesia's state flag carrier. Hubbing at Jakarta Soekarno-Hatta Airport, it operates domestic and international routes. Completed financial restructuring in 2022. Former Skytrax 5-Star recipient.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Garuda Indonesia captain salary in 2026?

Garuda Indonesia captain pay averages about ¥15M per year (range ¥10.5M–¥22M), pre-tax. Figures are reference values based on public data and industry benchmarks.

What is Garuda Indonesia first officer salary?

Garuda Indonesia first officer pay averages about ¥7.2M per year (range ¥4.7M–¥9.8M), pre-tax, rising with seniority — see the Career Ladder above for the year-by-year breakdown.

What are the requirements to become a Garuda Indonesia pilot?

Pilot Recruitment — Domestic & international.. Key requirements — License: ATPL (ICAO); English: ICAO Level 4+.

How does Garuda Indonesia pay compare to ANA or JAL?

Garuda Indonesia captains average ¥15M, versus roughly ¥27M at ANA / JAL. Both figures are pre-tax; Japan applies ~33% income tax.

What is Garuda Indonesia pilot salary per month?

Dividing the annual average by 12, Garuda Indonesia captains earn about ¥1.25M a month and first officers about ¥0.6M. What actually lands each month moves with block hours flown, and where an annual bonus is paid the regular monthly figure sits lower with a spike in the bonus month.

What is the Garuda Indonesia pilot pay scale?

Garuda Indonesia first officers average ¥7.2M and captains ¥15M — a step of about ¥7.8M, or roughly 2.1×. The full range runs ¥4.7M to ¥22M, and where you sit inside it is set by seniority, type rating and the routes you fly. The seniority table on this page breaks the same range into career stages.

Is Garuda Indonesia pilot salary before or after tax?

Every figure on this page is gross, before income tax. Captains average ¥15M and first officers ¥7.2M pre-tax, and income tax, social insurance and any local levies come out of that. Take-home depends on where you are resident and on your own deductions, so no single percentage applies. Airlines based in countries with no personal income tax — the UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia — leave more of the same gross figure in your hand.
