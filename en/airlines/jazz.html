FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Jazz Aviation captain salary in 2026?

We estimate Jazz Aviation captain pay at about $115K a year: the year-12 captain rate of C$158.83 an hour from 1 July 2026 (one table for all fleets) times 990 hours a year (the company’s 82.5-hour monthly average × 12).

What is Jazz Aviation first officer salary?

We estimate Jazz Aviation first officer pay at about $58K a year: the year-6 rate of C$80.58 an hour on the same table times the same 990 hours.

What is the Jazz Aviation pilot salary per month?

We do not publish a monthly figure for Jazz Aviation. An annual figure divided by twelve is not what lands in a given month — what you are paid moves with the hours you fly.

What is the Jazz Aviation pilot pay scale?

We estimate annual pay for Jazz Aviation captains at about $115K. That is our own estimate from public information, not a figure Jazz Aviation publishes. These are gross figures, before income tax. Is the Jazz Aviation pilot salary before or after tax?

Every figure on this page is gross, before income tax. Take-home depends on where you are resident and on your own deductions, so no single percentage applies, and we do not publish one. Airlines based in countries with no personal income tax — the UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia — leave more of the same gross figure in your hand.
