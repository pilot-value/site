Company

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the ANA Wings captain salary in 2026?

ANA Wings captain pay is estimated at about $126K per year, pre-tax. These are our own estimates from public information, not figures the airline publishes.

What is the ANA Wings first officer salary?

ANA Wings first officer pay is estimated at about $82K per year, pre-tax, rising with seniority.

What are the requirements to become an ANA Wings pilot?

ANA Wings Pilot Recruitment — First Officer (F/O) — experienced-hire and internal ANA Group transfer. Key requirements: Eligibility: Holders of a Commercial Pilot License (multi-engine, instrument), or internal ANA Group transfer; Base: Tokyo Haneda Airport (main base) and others; English: Aviation English Proficiency Level 4 or above. Recruitment status varies by period. Please check the ANA Group careers page or the official ANA Wings website.

How does ANA Wings pay compare to ANA or JAL?

ANA Wings captains, on our estimate, are on $126K, against roughly $170K at ANA / JAL. Both figures are pre-tax.

What is the ANA Wings pilot salary per month?

Dividing our estimated annual figures by 12, ANA Wings captains earn about $11K a month and first officers about $6.8K. What actually lands each month moves with block hours flown, and where an annual bonus is paid the regular monthly figure sits lower with a spike in the bonus month.

What is the ANA Wings pilot pay scale?

We estimate ANA Wings first officers at $82K and captains at $126K — a step of about $44K, or roughly 1.5×. The actual figure moves with seniority, type rating and the routes you fly.

Is the ANA Wings pilot salary before or after tax?

Every figure on this page is gross, before income tax. We estimate captains at $126K and first officers at $82K pre-tax, and income tax, social insurance and any local levies come out of that. Take-home depends on where you are resident and on your own deductions, so no single percentage applies. Airlines based in countries with no personal income tax — the UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia — leave more of the same gross figure in your hand.

Salary figures are our own estimates. Verify actual conditions on each airline's official website.
