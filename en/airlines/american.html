What is the American Airlines captain salary in 2026?

American Airlines captain pay is estimated at about $427K per year, pre-tax. These are our own estimates from public information, not figures the airline publishes.

What is the American Airlines first officer salary?

American Airlines first officer pay is estimated at about $265K per year, pre-tax, rising with seniority.

What are the requirements to become an American Airlines pilot?

First Officer — All Aircraft — B737/A321/B757/B767/B787/B777. Key requirements: License: ATP Certificate. We have not been able to confirm flight-hour requirements the airline publishes. US work authorization required. AA Cadet Academy pathway available.

What is the American Airlines pilot salary per month?

We do not publish a monthly figure for American Airlines. An annual figure divided by twelve is not what lands in a given month — what you are paid moves with the hours you fly.

What is the American Airlines pilot pay scale?

We estimate annual pay for American Airlines captains at about $427K. That is our own estimate from public information, not a figure American Airlines publishes. These are gross figures, before income tax. The actual figure moves with the aircraft type, years of service and the routes you fly. Is the American Airlines pilot salary before or after tax?

Every figure on this page is gross, before income tax. Take-home depends on where you are resident and on your own deductions, so no single percentage applies, and we do not publish one. Airlines based in countries with no personal income tax — the UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia — leave more of the same gross figure in your hand.
