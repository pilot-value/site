FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is FedEx Express captain salary in 2026?

We estimate FedEx Express captain pay at about $403K a year: the year-12 widebody (B777F / B767F / A300) captain rate of $456.35 an hour in the pilot contract that took effect on 29 June 2026, times 884 hours a year (the 68-hour minimum per four-week bid period, 13 times a year). Profit sharing, per diem and the company retirement plan are not included.

What is FedEx Express first officer salary?

We estimate FedEx Express first officer pay at about $250K a year: the year-6 widebody first officer rate of $283.40 an hour times the same 884 hours.

What are the requirements to fly for FedEx Express?

The careers page asks for an ATP certificate, a first-class medical, a radiotelephone operator permit, a valid US passport and SIDA badge eligibility, plus 1,500 hours total fixed-wing time with at least 500 hours PIC (1,000 hours preferred). FedEx does not sponsor work visas: non-US citizens need a Permanent Resident Card.

What is the FedEx Express pilot salary per month?

We do not publish a monthly figure for FedEx Express. An annual figure divided by twelve is not what lands in a given month — what you are paid moves with the hours you fly.

What is the FedEx Express pilot pay scale?

We estimate annual pay for FedEx Express captains at about $403K. That is our own estimate from public information, not a figure FedEx Express publishes. These are gross figures, before income tax. The actual figure moves with the aircraft type, years of service and the routes you fly. We estimate annual pay for FedEx Express first officers at about $250K. That is our own estimate from public information, not a figure FedEx Express publishes. These are gross figures, before income tax. The actual figure moves with the aircraft type, years of service and the routes you fly. Source checked 2026-11-02; yen figures are converted for comparison at the rate held as of 2026-08-22, not a live rate.

Is the FedEx Express pilot salary before or after tax?

Every figure on this page is gross, before income tax. Take-home depends on where you are resident and on your own deductions, so no single percentage applies, and we do not publish one. Airlines based in countries with no personal income tax — the UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia — leave more of the same gross figure in your hand.
