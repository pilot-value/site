Decision criteria

What separates the pilots who move to Emirates from those who don't

Profile of pilots who choose Emirates

  • ✦Maximizing take-home income is the number-one priority
  • ✦Fluent in English and comfortable in a foreign-carrier environment
  • ✦Able to bring the family (or single, or fine with an unaccompanied posting)
  • ✦Wants an environment where career progression is faster than in Japan
  • ✦Decides to move while still an ANA First Officer (annual salary 1,400–1,800万)
  • ✦Wants route experience across 150 cities worldwide

Profile of pilots who stay at ANA

  • ✦Values staying close to family and parents
  • ✦Prioritizes stability over the long road to Captain (15–18 years)
  • ✦Values the reassurance of Japan's pension and social insurance system
  • ✦Job security matters most — unwilling to take on business-performance risk
  • ✦Plans to live in Japan after retirement
  • ✦Prefers life in a Japanese-language environment and Japanese culture

Important: a move to Emirates is a one-way decision

ANA and JAL currently do not run mid-career hiring for serving pilots. If you move to Emirates, you need to make the decision on the assumption that, as a rule, there is no way back. A take-home gap of roughly 2x is compelling, but the call has to be made on the whole picture — family, retirement and your long-term life plan in Japan.

FAQ

ANA vs Emirates — frequently asked questions

How large is the take-home gap between ANA and Emirates?+
An ANA Captain (annual salary 2,700万円) takes home roughly 1,770万円. An Emirates Captain (annual salary 3,700万円, tax-free) keeps the full 3,700万円. That is a gap of about 1,930万円, and once housing and education allowances are included the effective package at Emirates works out at roughly 2.6x ANA's.
Can an ANA pilot move to Emirates?+
Yes. The requirements are ICAO Level 4 or above English, a current ATPL and the necessary flight hours (3,000+ hours for Captain). There is a substantial track record of Japanese pilots being hired. Applications usually go through a recruitment agency such as CAE or Parc Aviation.
Can you move to Emirates and later return to ANA?+
At present it is extremely difficult. Neither ANA nor JAL publicly runs mid-career hiring for serving pilots (CPL/ATPL holders). Treat a move to Emirates as a one-way decision and weigh it against your total life plan.
What would an ANA First Officer (annual salary 1,800万円) earn after moving to Emirates?+
An ANA First Officer joining Emirates as an F/O can expect roughly 2,800万円 (tax-free) as a guide. Against an ANA First Officer's take-home of about 1,230万円, the entire 2,800万円 at Emirates is take-home — an increase of roughly 2.3x in what actually lands in your account.
Will an Emirates pilot's retirement pension be secure?+
Japanese employees' pension contributions stop while you are posted overseas (voluntary enrollment in the National Pension remains possible). You need a plan to channel the higher earnings you make at Emirates into investment and wealth-building, accumulating your own retirement assets. Putting that high income to work through vehicles such as iDeCo (Japan's individual defined-contribution pension) and overseas investments becomes essential.
