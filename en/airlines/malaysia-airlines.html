About Malaysia Airlines

Malaysia Airlines (MAB) hubs at Kuala Lumpur International Airport. Post the 2014 dual tragedies (MH370, MH17), the airline restructured and continues operations today as a Oneworld member. Has a history of foreign captain recruitment.

Pilot pay — under review

Malaysia Airlines does not publish what it pays its pilots. It does not currently advertise for pilots at all. The three entry points on its careers page are cabin crew, engineering and other roles, and going through the 27 open postings on its own recruitment system one by one turns up no captain, first officer or cadet vacancy. The only line that touches pay says the package is “competitive and in line with industry standards”, with no amount attached.
The average and range this page used to show had no record of the document they were built from. Until we can confirm a source, the published figure is marked under review. The old value has not been deleted — it is kept in our change history.

What is the Malaysia Airlines pilot pay scale?

We estimate annual pay for Malaysia Airlines captains at about $120K, with a range of roughly $82K–$164K. That is our own estimate from public information, not a figure Malaysia Airlines publishes. These are gross figures, before income tax. Where you sit inside the range is set by the aircraft type, years of service and the routes you fly. Is Malaysia Airlines pilot salary before or after tax?

Every figure on this page is gross, before income tax. Take-home depends on where you are resident and on your own deductions, so no single percentage applies, and we do not publish one.
