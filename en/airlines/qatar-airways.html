About Qatar Airways

Qatar Airways, founded 1993, is the state-owned flag carrier of Qatar. Hubbing at Hamad International Airport (HIA) in Doha, it serves 160+ cities in 60+ countries as a Oneworld core member.

Qatar has NO personal income tax — 100% of salary is take-home. Add housing, education, and transportation allowances and this becomes one of the most popular overseas destinations for Japanese pilots.

Training

Training & Checkrides

Type Rating (Direct Entry)

Qatar Airways Flight Training Center (QAFTC) in Doha. World-class modern facility.

Life in Doha

Doha is rapidly developing. English widely spoken. International schools and Japanese food available. Safe environment.

Captain Upgrade

Typically 3–7 years as F/O. Direct Entry Captains also actively recruited. Based on performance and vacancies.

Recurrent Checks

Proficiency checks 1–2x per year. International-standard training quality.

Hours Guarantee

75h/month guaranteed. Basic pay protected even if below guarantee.

Training Costs

Company covers type rating on joining (bond applies). Departure before term triggers repayment obligation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Qatar Airways captain salary in 2026?

Qatar Airways captain pay averages about ¥29M per year (range ¥26M–¥48M), 100% tax-free (no personal income tax). Figures are reference values based on public data and industry benchmarks.

What is Qatar Airways first officer salary?

Qatar Airways first officer pay averages about ¥20.5M per year (range ¥18.5M–¥27M), 100% tax-free (no personal income tax), rising with seniority — see the Career Ladder above for the year-by-year breakdown.

Is Qatar Airways salary really tax-free?

Yes — there is no personal income tax in the home country, so pilots keep 100% of gross pay. For a Japanese pilot that is roughly 1.5× the take-home of an equivalent ANA / JAL salary, which is taxed around 33%.

How does Qatar Airways pay compare to ANA or JAL?

Qatar Airways captains average ¥29M tax-free, versus roughly ¥27M pre-tax at ANA / JAL (taxed ~33%). After tax the overseas gap is even wider.

What is Qatar Airways pilot salary per month?

Dividing the annual average by 12, Qatar Airways captains earn about ¥2.42M a month and first officers about ¥1.71M. What actually lands each month moves with block hours flown, and where an annual bonus is paid the regular monthly figure sits lower with a spike in the bonus month.

What is the Qatar Airways pilot pay scale?

Qatar Airways first officers average ¥20.5M and captains ¥29M — a step of about ¥8.5M, or roughly 1.4×. The full range runs ¥18.5M to ¥48M, and where you sit inside it is set by seniority, type rating and the routes you fly. The seniority table on this page breaks the same range into career stages.
