What are the requirements to become a Frontier Airlines pilot?

Captain / First Officer — Domestic and international flying. Primarily Denver-based. Key requirements: License: FAA ATP. Frontier’s pilot recruitment page asks for an ATP, US work authorization and 1,500 hours total time (2,500 hours preferred). Applicants holding an A320-family Type Rating are given preference.

We do not publish a monthly figure for Frontier Airlines. An annual figure divided by twelve is not what lands in a given month — what you are paid moves with the hours you fly.
