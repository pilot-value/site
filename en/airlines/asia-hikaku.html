SINGAPORE

Singapore Airlines publishes its requirements in detail — and no remuneration section at all

What the postings state

Captain (contract, up to 12 months)
7,000 total hours, including 3,000 hours PIC on international commercial jets and 1,500 hours PIC on the A350 or A380, plus two years as captain on type.
First officer
3,000 total hours preferred, 1,500 acceptable — but 1,500 hours on the A350 or A380 and two years on type are required.
English
ICAO Level 5+ — the strictest of the seven
Cadet bond
Seven years from appointment as first officer

A published minimum height

“Minimum height requirement of 1.58m to ensure full reach and the safe operation of all flight deck controls”
⚠️ There is no remuneration section
Neither the captain nor the first officer posting has a section on pay. There are three: selection process, training and tenure of service. The only wording is "more than just a competitive salary" and, for cadets, "an attractive salary" — no figures. The number of annual leave days is not stated either.
Source: Singapore Airlines posting (direct entry captains, contract)
⚠️ Do not apply Singapore Airlines’ requirements to Scoot

Scoot’s careers site refuses every kind of visitor we tried, and archived copies were not retrievable. All we could confirm, from search listings, were the vacancy titles: "Direct Entry A320 Captain 2026 (Singapore Assessment Centre)", "Direct Entry First Officer 2026" and "Direct Entry B787 Captain 2026". This page therefore states no flight hours, English level, age, medical or pay under Scoot’s name. Being in the same group is not a reason to borrow another airline’s requirements.

Page attempted: careers.flyscoot.com
VIETNAM

Both Vietnamese carriers file annual reports — but the numbers cover all employees

VN

Vietnam Airlines — the only one of the seven to publish its pilot headcount

Operating pilots as at 31 December 2025, including VASCO: 1,055 — 532 captains and 523 first officers. Vietnamese nationals 966 (91.60%), foreign nationals 99 (9.40%).
Source: Vietnam Airlines annual report 2025 (Vietnamese)
  • In 2025, 20 first officers were upgraded to captain — 100% of a plan of 20 — and 112 pilots completed type transitions. No other carrier here publishes upgrade numbers.
  • ⚠️ On pay there are only rates: average income up more than 25% and average wages up nearly 40% against 2019. These cover all employees, not pilots.
  • Hiring happens on a separate recruitment site, not in the report. We checked all 21 vacancies one by one: no pilot opening, and every row lists pay as "negotiable".
Vacancy list: skyhr.vietnamairlines.com/jobs
VJ

VietJet Air — there is an average-income chart, and three reasons not to use it

The 2025 annual report charts average income per person per month. Headcount is 7,632, and the job mix is stated as cabin crew 27.92%, pilots 12.47% and other staff 59.60%.

⚠️ Why this chart is not used as pilot pay
  1. It covers all employees, and pilots are only 12.47% of headcount.
  2. The report does not label which bar belongs to which year. We could only tie the 7,632 headcount to 2025; the other years could not be verified.
  3. It does not define the difference between "thu nhập" (income) and "tiền lương" (wages), so what the amount includes is unknown.
What is certain is the unit: the chart says "/tháng" — per month.
⚠️ There are no vacancies — the careers site shows "Pilots (0)". The 4,000 hours in the table comes from a posting opened 14 February 2025 and closed on 17 February: captains 4,000 total hours, 1,500 hours PIC multi-crew (excluding PICUS), 1,500 hours A320F and 500 hours PIC A320F; first officers 4,000 total and 1,500 hours A320F. That posting carried a "Salary:" heading with nothing after it.

CAREER GUIDE

Which Asian carrier to approach

1
You want a figure before moving, or your hours are low
Cathay Pacific

The only one of the seven where the target annual salary, monthly allowance, annual leave and hour minimums all sit on one page. Second officers can enter from 250 hours, the lowest door here. Captain pay, though, is not published.

2
You have wide-body captain time and strong English
Singapore Airlines

Requirements are published in detail, and A350 or A380 captain time counts directly. The English bar, ICAO Level 5+, is the highest of the seven. But the postings contain no pay section at all, so the figure has to be established during selection. The contract is stated as up to 12 months.

3
You are looking for "the highest-paying Asian carrier"
It cannot be settled officially

One of the seven publishes a figure, and even that one omits captains. Any article that ranks them has mixed an estimate in somewhere. What this site can give is each carrier’s range and where it came from.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which Asian airline pays pilots the most?+
Among the seven carriers on this page, Cathay Pacific captains sit at $189K–$355K, Singapore Airlines captains at $173K–$261K, Scoot captains at $120K–$182K, HK Express captains at $88K–$164K, Vietnam Airlines captains at $88K–$157K, VietJet Air captains at $75K–$145K and Philippine Airlines captains at $72K–$132K. Only one of the seven publishes a figure at all — and not for captains.
Does any Asian airline publish pilot pay?+
Only Cathay Pacific. Its recruitment page gives a Target Annual Salary of HKD 964,646 for first officers and HKD 629,109 for second officers, alongside monthly allowances (from HKD 20,000 and HKD 14,000) and annual leave (28 and 21 days). The figures carry the footnote "Latest figures as of Jan 2026, subject to periodic review".
Is Cathay Pacific captain pay published?+
No. The recruitment page carries figures for cadets, second officers and first officers only; there is no captain figure. The captain range shown on this page comes from anonymous first-hand data and public material, with no official backing.
Is the Target Annual Salary guaranteed?+
No. Cathay Pacific’s page states "Target Annual Salary based on achieving Target Annual Block Hours" — it moves with the hours actually flown. On tax, the same page says "Typical Hong Kong tax rate at around 17%".
Which Asian carriers are hiring pilots right now?+
As of September 2026 we could confirm openings at Cathay Pacific (cadet, second officer, first officer), Singapore Airlines (captain and first officer; direct entry second officer is closed) and Scoot (A320 captain, first officer, B787 captain). HK Express had only one internal line training captain posting, and Vietnam Airlines, VietJet Air and Philippine Airlines had no pilot vacancy at all.
Anything unusual in Singapore Airlines’ requirements?+
A published minimum height: "Minimum height requirement of 1.58m to ensure full reach and the safe operation of all flight deck controls." Its English requirement is ICAO Level 5 or above, the strictest in the region. Captains need 7,000 total hours, including 3,000 hours PIC on international commercial jets and 1,500 hours PIC on the A350 or A380, plus two years as captain on type.
Do the Vietnamese carriers’ annual reports state pilot pay?+
No. Vietnam Airlines reports only company-wide growth rates — average income up more than 25% and average wages up nearly 40% against 2019. VietJet’s annual report charts average income per person per month, but again for all employees, with pilots making up 12.47% of headcount. Neither can be used as pilot pay.
How many pilots does Vietnam Airlines employ?+
As of 31 December 2025, 1,055 including VASCO: 532 captains and 523 first officers, of whom 966 (91.60%) are Vietnamese nationals and 99 (9.40%) are foreign. It is the only one of these seven carriers that publishes its pilot headcount. In 2025, 20 first officers were upgraded to captain (100% of a plan of 20) and 112 pilots completed type transitions.
