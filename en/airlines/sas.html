About SAS Scandinavian Airlines

SAS Scandinavian Airlines is the flag carrier jointly funded by three countries — Sweden, Denmark, and Norway. From its hubs in Copenhagen, Stockholm, and Oslo, it serves all of Europe as well as North America and Asia, and it is a member of Star Alliance. In 2022 the airline filed for protection under U.S. Chapter 11 bankruptcy law, and after going through restructuring proceedings it relaunched in 2024. It is currently being rebuilt under the ownership of Aqua Partners (Castard and Apollo).

Routes & Fleet

Routes

From its hubs in Copenhagen, Stockholm, and Oslo, SAS serves North America (New York, Chicago, etc.), Asia (Tokyo, Shanghai, Bangkok, etc.), and all of Europe.

Fleet

Airbus A350-900, A330-300, A321LR/neo, A320neo. Approximately 130 aircraft (fleet under restructuring).

Training

Training & Checkrides

Type Rating (EASA-approved ATO)

Type rating obtained at an EASA-certified Approved Training Organization (ATO). Ground school → simulator (including MCC) → LIFUS.

LIFUS (Line Training)

After obtaining the type rating, Line Flying Under Supervision is conducted with an instructor captain on board. Typically around 50–80 legs (in accordance with EASA FCL.060).

Recurrent Checks (OPC/LPC)

Proficiency Checks (PC) once or twice a year, to EASA FCL.625/735 standards. Supervised by the respective national CAAs (CAA UK / DGAC / LBA, etc.).

Captain Upgrade & Additional Ratings

After a period as SFO / Senior F/O, pilots undergo captain training. Additional qualifications such as LVP (Low Visibility Procedures), PBN, ETOPS, and RVSM are also obtained.

Benefits

Pilot Benefits & Allowances

✈️
Staff Travel

Heavily discounted or free tickets for the employee and family. Also usable on alliance partner carriers.

🏥
Health Insurance

High-standard European health insurance. Loss-of-license insurance is also common.

💰
Bonus

Performance-linked bonus (equivalent to 2–4 months' salary per year). In some cases tied to individual performance evaluations.

📅
Paid Leave

25–35 days per year. Generous leave provisions under European labor law (EU directives).

🏦
Retirement / Corporate Pension

Defined-benefit or defined-contribution corporate pension (DBP/DCP). In Europe this operates as a dual structure alongside the state pension.

🌐
Layover Allowance

Accommodation and per diem paid for overseas stays. For major European cities, the high-cost-area allowance tends to be higher.

🌿
Nordic Welfare Model

Benefits from the three Nordic countries' comprehensive public healthcare, parental leave, and pension systems. Income tax is high, but social security is generous.

How does SAS Scandinavian Airlines pay compare to ANA or JAL?

SAS Scandinavian Airlines captains average ¥26.5M, versus roughly ¥27M at ANA / JAL. Both figures are pre-tax; Japan applies ~33% income tax.

What is SAS Scandinavian Airlines pilot salary per month?

Dividing the annual average by 12, SAS Scandinavian Airlines captains earn about ¥2.21M a month and first officers about ¥0.96M. What actually lands each month moves with block hours flown, and where an annual bonus is paid the regular monthly figure sits lower with a spike in the bonus month.

What is the SAS Scandinavian Airlines pilot pay scale?

SAS Scandinavian Airlines first officers average ¥11.5M and captains ¥26.5M — a step of about ¥15M, or roughly 2.3×. The full range runs ¥7.4M to ¥31.7M, and where you sit inside it is set by seniority, type rating and the routes you fly. The seniority table on this page breaks the same range into career stages.

Is SAS Scandinavian Airlines pilot salary before or after tax?

Every figure on this page is gross, before income tax. Captains average ¥26.5M and first officers ¥11.5M pre-tax, and income tax, social insurance and any local levies come out of that. Take-home depends on where you are resident and on your own deductions, so no single percentage applies. Airlines based in countries with no personal income tax — the UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia — leave more of the same gross figure in your hand.
