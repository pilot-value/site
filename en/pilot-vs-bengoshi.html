INCOME REALITY

What makes the pilot route hard

Company-sponsored cadet programs attract several hundred applicants per place. Get in, however, and you qualify at zero cost. There is also an alternative route via a private flight school (1,000–1,500万円). The difficulty is at the door — once you are through it, the airline trains you.

What makes the lawyer route hard

The bar exam itself has a pass rate of 37–40%, but completing law school is the prerequisite for getting there. Total costs exceed 500万円 even at a national university. Both the long 9–10 year run-up and the heavy financial burden are barriers.

CAREER FREEDOM

Independence and freedom of career

It is not only the income ceiling that differs — so does the freedom to decide how you work.

Lawyers — a high degree of freedom

✓
You can set up your own practice

Run your own firm and control your own income ceiling. Succeed, and there is no limit.

✓
You choose your specialism freely

M&A, criminal, IP, medical, IT law — you can specialize according to your interests or where the market demand is.

✓

Qualify at the New York bar and you can work on international matters. With the language skills, a move to an international firm or to London is on the table.

✓

Advisory retainers, speaking engagements, writing books — you can build several income streams.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who earns more, a pilot or a lawyer?+
On average, pilots at 1,697万円 are far ahead of lawyers at 738万円 (JFBA 2023 survey). That said, the top 10% of lawyers clear 2,000万円, and partners earning more than 1億円 are not unusual. Compared on averages, pilots win decisively; measured by how high you can climb, law holds considerable potential.
Why are lawyers' salaries falling?+
The law school system introduced in 2006 drove a rapid increase in the number of lawyers, with roughly 45,000 now registered. Supply has grown faster than demand and competition has intensified. The decline is most pronounced among junior and regional lawyers, and salaries of 300–500万円 are no longer rare.
Which is harder, becoming a pilot or becoming a lawyer?+
Both are extremely demanding. The bar exam pass rate is 37–40%, but law school is a prerequisite and the whole process takes 9–10 years of preparation. Company-sponsored cadet programs draw several hundred applicants per place, though the private flight school route (1,000–1,500万円) is an alternative. The nature of the difficulty differs, which makes a straight comparison hard.
Can lawyers go independent? What about pilots?+
Lawyers can set up on their own, and with their own firm there is no ceiling on income. For pilots, independence is effectively impossible — the job assumes employment by an airline, and outside work is often prohibited. If you want to build your own business or remove the ceiling on your income, law has an overwhelming advantage.
Which has the better long-term outlook, pilot or lawyer?+
The global pilot shortage continues, and demand is widely expected to keep growing into the 2030s, so the outlook is strong. Lawyers face the risk that AI and legal tech will take over routine work such as contract review and statutory research. High-level negotiation, litigation strategy and complex judgment calls will remain. On long-term stability, pilots currently have the edge.
