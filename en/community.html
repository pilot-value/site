How we protect your anonymity and safety
  • • Exact ages are never shown — we display bands such as "early 30s" or "40s"
  • • Your employer is chosen from a list — free-text wording that could identify a company is not allowed
  • • Specific routes and bases are hidden — only the "international" / "domestic" distinction is published
  • • Reviews that could identify an individual are not published — we check them through moderation
  • • We do not log IP addresses — anonymous posting is guaranteed technically
