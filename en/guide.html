ABOUT

What is PILOT VALUE?

PILOT VALUE is an information platform that lets you compare annual salary, hiring and training data for pilots flying with airlines in Japan and around the world, all in one place. Our aim is to provide accurate, genuinely useful information to aspiring pilots, pilots considering a career move, and anyone planning their long-term career path.

How to use the information on this site

  • Each airline page shows the annual salary range and average for Captains and First Officers, the main fleet types, and route information.
  • The "World Airlines" page lets you narrow results using the map, regional filters and the search function.
  • The salary rankings let you compare by region and by airline.
  • Country pages summarise typical pilot pay in that country alongside its main airlines.

About the accuracy of our data

The salary data published on this site consists of reference figures based on airlines' publicly available information, industry reports and information from the pilot community. Actual pay and conditions vary according to each company's hiring terms, an individual's years of experience, and the time of application.

Before making any final decision about a career move or a job application, always confirm the details directly with the airline's official recruitment page or hiring contact.

Accounts and member features

  • Free membership registration lets you join the community and post information.
  • All you need to register is an email address.
  • You can change your password at any time.
  • You can request account deletion from your profile page.

Contact us

Please send us your opinions and requests about the site, or reports of incorrect information, via the community page or our official social media accounts. We use the feedback we receive to improve the site.
