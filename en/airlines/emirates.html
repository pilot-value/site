Overview

About Emirates

Emirates was founded in Dubai (UAE) in 1985 and is the flag carrier of the United Arab Emirates. Operating from Dubai International Airport (DXB) as its hub, Emirates serves 150+ cities across 6 continents.

The biggest advantage: the UAE has zero personal income tax, so cash pay is received with no income tax deducted. The airline's own direct-entry captain posting quotes a total package of AED 1,185,000 a year (about $323K) with housing, transport, school fees and medical cover valued into it. Of that, AED 575,000 a year (about $157K) is cash pay. These are recruitment examples, not a company-wide average.

Operations

Routes & Fleet

Routes

150+ destinations from Dubai (DXB). Multiple daily flights to Japan (NRT, HND, KIX, NGO, FUK). 100% international routes — no domestic flying. Hub-and-spoke through DXB with 1,000+ daily departures.

Fleet

A380, B777-300ER (primary long-haul workhorse), B777X (on order), A350 (on order, deliveries from 2024)

Training

Training & Checkrides

Type Rating (Direct Entry)

For pilots with existing licenses. B777 or A380 type rating at Emirates Flight Training Academy (EFTA), Dubai. Duration ~3 months. Company-sponsored.

Emirates Flight Training Academy

Training facility with all type simulators in-house.

Captain Upgrade

Typically 5–8 years as F/O before upgrading, subject to vacancies and performance evaluation. Direct Entry Captain recruitment is also common.

Life in Dubai

English is widely used, with a large international community. Japanese schools and grocery stores available. Low crime rate.

Benefits

Pilot Benefits & Allowances

🚫💰
Zero Income Tax

The UAE has 0% personal income tax. Every dirham of your salary is take-home pay — a decisive advantage over Japan.

🏠
Housing Allowance

Captain: AED 16,075/month (~$5.9K/month). First Officer: AED 14,325/month. Or a company-provided 3–4 bedroom villa with utilities included.

🎓
Education Allowance

Up to 3 children aged 4–19. Primary: AED 42,750/year per child. Secondary: AED 65,250/year per child. Covers Dubai international schools.

✈️
Travel Benefits

1 confirmed annual return home-leave ticket (business class). Unlimited concessional tickets for spouse + children. 15 extended-family tickets/year.

🏥
Medical & Dental

Full medical and dental coverage for pilot + all dependants, worldwide network. Full pay for up to 52 weeks sick leave (after 6-month probation).

📋
Loss of License Insurance

Coverage up to 36 months of base salary for permanent license loss due to accident or illness. Essential protection for every commercial pilot.

💼
Provident Fund

Employee: 5% of base salary. Company: 12% for years 1–10, 15% from year 10+. Vests at 75% after 3–5 years, 100% after 5+ years.

📈
Profit Sharing

2–24 weeks of base salary paid in May when company performance targets are met. Historically paid most years (requires full Apr 1–Mar 31 employment).

🌟
Annual Leave

42 calendar days per year (minimum 30 guaranteed under UAE law). Scheduling via roster management with fair rotation.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Emirates captain salary in 2026?

Emirates publishes a direct-entry captain recruitment example rather than an average: a total package of AED 1,185,000 a year (about $323K) that already contains housing, transport, school fees, medical cover, insurance, leave tickets and the company's provident-fund contribution, of which AED 575,000 a year (about $157K) is cash pay. The UAE has no personal income tax, so the cash pay is received with no income tax deducted. It is not an all-staff average or a guaranteed minimum. Source checked 2026-09-26.

What is Emirates first officer salary?

Emirates publishes first officer recruitment examples rather than an average. Standard: total package AED 900,000 a year (about $245K), of which AED 385,000 (about $105K) is cash pay. Enhanced, for first officers who meet the stated experience and licence conditions: total package AED 935,000 a year (about $255K), of which AED 415,000 (about $113K) is cash pay. Enhanced is not a figure that applies to all first officers. Source checked 2026-09-26.

Is Emirates salary really tax-free?

The United Arab Emirates has no personal income tax, so the cash pay Emirates quotes is received with no income tax deducted. Japanese airline salaries are quoted before tax is taken off, so the same headline figure leaves a different amount in hand. The ANA and JAL figures on this site are our own estimates, while Emirates quotes a figure from its own posting, so we do not publish a multiple between them. Your own tax position, Japanese residency in particular, needs checking separately.

What are the requirements to become a Emirates pilot?

Emirates publishes its entry requirements on its careers site. Direct Entry Captain — 7,000+ total flight hours and ICAO English Level 5 or above. First Officer, Standard — 2,000+ total flight hours and ICAO English Level 4 or above. First Officer, Enhanced — 4,000+ total flight hours on jets above 20 t MTOW, plus 2,000+ hours on multi-crew jets above 50 t MTOW, and ICAO English Level 5 or above. ※ Work visa fully sponsored by Emirates. Pilots of all nationalities hired. Applications commonly submitted via recruitment consultants.

What is Emirates pilot salary per month?

Direct entry captain: cash pay, monthly $13K; First officer, Standard: cash pay, monthly $8.7K; First officer, Enhanced: cash pay, monthly $9.4K. These are the monthly figures the airline publishes, not an annual figure divided by twelve. Twelve times the monthly figure does not equal the published annual figure, and we leave both as published. A total package that includes housing and school fees divided by twelve is not a monthly salary.

What is the Emirates pilot pay scale?

For captains we publish a official posting rather than an average — Direct entry captain: cash pay, annual $157K, total package, annual (includes the cash pay) $323K. This is not an all-staff average. For first officers we publish a official posting rather than an average — First officer, Standard: cash pay, annual $105K, total package, annual (includes the cash pay) $245K; First officer, Enhanced: cash pay, annual $113K, total package, annual (includes the cash pay) $255K. This is not an all-staff average. The cash figure is the airline’s own "take home cash". It is a recruitment example built on basic pay plus a flying allowance for 85 hours a month.
