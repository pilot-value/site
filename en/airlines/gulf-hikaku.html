READING THE OFFICIAL NUMBERS

Emirates publishes three numbers, and which one you call "salary" changes it by over 2×

Emirates publishes the most detail of any Gulf carrier. But the three figures it lists do not add up to one another. Aggregator sites can quote a single "Emirates captain earns X" only because they never say which of the three they used.

★ What each figure covers, per the conditions on the Emirates page
  • Take home cash — basic salary and flying pay, annualised at 85 flying hours a month.
  • Pay & benefits — the cash above plus accommodation, education, medical, travel and provident fund, and it assumes "employee, spouse and 2 eligible children". Someone without children does not receive the same amount.
  • Accommodation value — the value of the housing provided in kind. It does not arrive as cash.

The Emirates range on this page sits above the take-home-cash figure alone and well below the pay-and-benefits total, because the pay reports we collect are gross pay as printed on a payslip. We have deliberately not replaced the range with the official figures: the definitions do not line up, so the official numbers stay marked as candidates in our source ledger until they do.

ETIHAD

Etihad states an "up to" figure, with its conditions, in the job posting

First Officer

“with the potential to earn up to AED 513,732 (USD 138,318) per year based on 75 flying hours per month”
Source: Etihad Airways job posting (first officer)
  • What it contains: basic salary, flying pay, housing allowance, and layover and meal allowances.
  • What it excludes: education allowance (AED 40,000 primary, AED 55,000 secondary, up to three children), income protection, insurance, staff travel, end-of-service benefit and relocation support — all listed separately.
  • Requirements: 2,000 hours total with 1,500 hours multi-crew glass cockpit and 500 hours on type (criteria A), or 1,500 hours total with 1,000 hours on A320 (criteria B). A320 flying within the last 12 months, ICAO ATPL (frozen accepted), class one medical, under 50 on joining, ICAO English Level 4+. 42 days annual leave.

Captain

“up to AED 674,029 (USD 181,589) per year based on 75 flying hours per month”
Source: Etihad Airways job posting (captain, ref 80371)
  • Requirements: 5,500 hours total, 2,500 hours PIC multi-crew glass cockpit, 1,500 hours PIC on A320. A320 flying within the last 12 months, ATPL, class one medical, under 59 on joining, ICAO English Level 4+. Augmented flights are counted at 75%, stated explicitly. 42 days annual leave.
  • ⚠️ This "up to" figure lands slightly below the bottom of the captain range on this page. The gap is about 3%. The likely reasons are the assumption of 75 flying hours a month and the separately listed items such as education and end-of-service benefit. We show both as they are rather than moving one to match.
★ Housing in kind and a cash housing allowance are not the same item

Emirates provides accommodation in kind and states its value apart from pay. Etihad pays a cash housing allowance, which sits inside the "up to AED 513,732". So although both are "housing included", the Emirates take-home-cash figure excludes housing while the Etihad figure includes it. Add or subtract across the two and one of them always comes out wrong. This is the main reason aggregator comparison tables of the Gulf disagree with each other.

Etihad also publishes cadet pay as monthly amounts — starting at AED 5,000, then 16,667, then 24,000, capped at 34,000, and AED 39,853 on appointment as senior first officer. These are monthly, not annual, so they cannot be lined up directly against other carriers’ annual figures.

WHERE NOTHING OFFICIAL EXISTS

Three carriers publish nothing. We leave it empty rather than fill it in

For these three, neither pay nor requirements can be read officially. The table could be filled by borrowing figures from other sites, but that would make every other cell untrustworthy too, so it stays empty.

QR

Qatar Airways — not one official document obtained

Our source ledger contains no official Qatar Airways document at all. There is no verified official wording for pay, flight-hour minimums, English levels, age limits or housing. That is why this page states no Qatar Airways figure other than the pay range, which comes from anonymous first-hand data and public material.

RX

Riyadh Air — the vacancies are withdrawn and the site refuses to be read

  • The careers site refuses every kind of visitor we tried — ordinary browsers, search-engine and mobile identities, with and without www. Archived copies were not retrievable either.
  • The two pilot requisitions that used to exist now both redirect to the generic careers page, meaning they have been withdrawn. The talent-community form lists only Human Resources.
  • Only one sentence was recoverable, and it is not about pay: "Riyadh Air will only engage with potential applicants through our official channels and that Riyadh Air would never request any payments or personal bank details during the application process."
⚠️ This page states none of Riyadh Air’s requirements
Flight hours, licences, English level, age, medical and the tax-free package are all absent because none could be verified officially. The pay range lands in the highest band of the five Gulf carriers, but it is built from anonymous first-hand data and public material with no official backing whatsoever. Read it as a range.
Page attempted: riyadhair.com/en/careers/pilots
GF

Gulf Air — there is no vacancy at all

  • We checked all ten vacancies on the careers page one by one. There is no pilot, captain or first officer opening.
  • On pay, the page says only "Competitive salary package." No figure appears.
  • The GAPTU collective agreement has only a July 2024 announcement; neither the text nor any figure is published. The union’s own site is essentially an empty placeholder.
  • ⚠️ Applications have moved to a separate recruitment system. The jobs.gulfair.com and careers.gulfair.com addresses that appear in search results no longer resolve at all. Do not trust links in older articles.
Source: Gulf Air careers page
CAREER GUIDE

Which Gulf carrier to approach

1
You are moving with family and want housing and school fees in the comparison
Emirates

The only one of the five that states the value of company accommodation as a figure. It also publishes a total that assumes "employee, spouse and 2 eligible children", so the closer your family is to that assumption, the more readable it is on paper. But the captain door is also the highest of the five: 7,000 hours and ICAO English Level 5+.

2
Your hours are still short, or you fly the A320
Etihad Airways

It publishes two doors for first officers, and states officially that 1,500 total hours is acceptable with 1,000 hours on the A320. Age limits (under 50 for first officers, under 59 for captains), the figure and the requirements all sit in one posting, which makes it the easiest of the five to verify.

3
You are looking for "the highest-paying Gulf carrier"
It cannot be settled officially

Only two of the five publish a figure, and those two are not counting the same things (housing in kind versus a cash allowance). Any article that gives a ranking has mixed an estimate in somewhere. What this site can give is each carrier’s range and where that range came from. The only way to narrow it is more data from pilots actually flying there.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which Gulf airline pays pilots the most?+
In the data collected on this site, Riyadh Air captains sit at $226K–$315K, Emirates captains at $211K–$318K, Etihad Airways captains at $189K–$377K, Qatar Airways captains at $164K–$302K and Gulf Air captains at $145K–$208K. Only two of the five — Emirates and Etihad — publish any pay figure at all; the other three publish none. There is no defensible "average" figure.
Do Gulf airlines publish pilot pay?+
Only Emirates and Etihad Airways. Emirates lists three figures for each rank — annual pay and benefits, annual take home cash, and a monthly amount — in both AED and USD. Etihad states in its job postings that a first officer can earn "up to AED 513,732 (USD 138,318) per year based on 75 flying hours per month". Qatar Airways, Riyadh Air and Gulf Air publish no figure on their own sites.
What is the difference between the three Emirates figures?+
They cover different things. Annual take home cash is basic salary plus flying pay annualised at 85 flying hours a month — the cash that actually arrives. Annual pay and benefits adds company housing, education, medical, travel and provident fund on top, and assumes "employee, spouse and 2 eligible children". The value of the housing itself is stated separately again. The three do not add up to one another, so which one you call "salary" changes the number by more than a factor of two.
How does housing differ between Emirates and Etihad?+
In kind versus cash. Emirates provides company accommodation and states its value separately (AED 290,000 for captains, AED 225,000 for first officers). Etihad pays a cash housing allowance, and that allowance sits inside the "up to AED 513,732" figure together with basic salary, flying pay and layover and meal allowances. Because one is in kind and the other is cash, they cannot be added or subtracted as if they were the same item.
Is the Gulf really tax-free?+
Etihad states "tax-free" explicitly in its job postings, in the sense that the country of employment does not deduct income tax. Whether it is tax-free for you also depends on your nationality and residence status, so what the official pages claim only goes as far as "no income tax withheld locally". The ranges on this page are treated as amounts with no income tax deducted.
What are Riyadh Air’s pilot requirements?+
They cannot be read officially right now. As of September 2026 the careers site refuses automated access, and the two pilot requisitions that used to exist both redirect to the generic careers page, meaning the vacancies have been withdrawn. For that reason this page states no flight hours, licence, English level, age limit, medical or tax-free package under Riyadh Air’s name. What cannot be verified officially is not written.
Is Gulf Air hiring pilots?+
Not as of September 2026. We checked all ten vacancies on its careers page one by one and found no pilot, captain or first officer opening. On pay, the page says only "Competitive salary package." — no figure. On the union side, the GAPTU collective agreement has a July 2024 announcement but no published figures.
Where do the Qatar Airways numbers come from?+
From first-hand data submitted anonymously to this site, together with public material. Qatar Airways publishes no pay figure on its own site, and our source ledger contains no official Qatar Airways document at all. That is why this page shows a range only.
