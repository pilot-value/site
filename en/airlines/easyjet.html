About easyJet

easyJet is a low-cost carrier founded with its base at London Luton Airport, and today it connects more than 150 cities across the whole of Europe. It maintains major bases in London, Amsterdam, Geneva, Milan and elsewhere. Its fleet is standardized on the A320 family. Following Brexit, easyJet established easyJet Europe as an EU subsidiary to preserve its European route network. Pilots require a UK CAA / EASA ATPL.

Operations

Routes & Fleet

Routes

Operates from multiple bases — including London, Amsterdam, Geneva, Madrid and Milan — to destinations across the whole of Europe, North Africa and the Middle East. Specializes in short- and medium-haul routes.

Fleet

Airbus A321neo, A320neo, A320ceo and A319. Around 350 aircraft.

Training

Training & Checkrides

Type Rating (EASA-approved — note on cost sharing)

Conducted at EASA-approved ATOs. At LCCs, some contract structures require the pilot to bear part or all of the type-rating cost, so the terms should be confirmed.

LIFUS

Line training under a supervising instructor captain after obtaining the type rating. Because LCCs fly many short-haul sectors, pilots can build up their leg counts relatively quickly.

Recurrent Checks (OPC/LPC)

Checks are held once or twice a year in line with EASA standards. LCCs apply the same safety standards as full-service carriers.

Upgrade

Upgrade from First Officer to Captain typically requires a minimum of 4,000–5,000+ flight hours as a guideline. LCCs can also offer more frequent upgrade opportunities.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is easyJet captain salary in 2026?

easyJet captain pay averages about ¥27M per year (range ¥23M–¥36M), pre-tax. Figures are reference values based on public data and industry benchmarks.

What is easyJet first officer salary?

easyJet first officer pay averages about ¥15M per year (range ¥12.1M–¥19.8M), pre-tax, rising with seniority — see the Career Ladder above for the year-by-year breakdown.

What are the requirements to become a easyJet pilot?

Captain / First Officer (continuous recruitment) — European route flying. Choice of multiple bases.. Key requirements — Required license: UK CAA / EASA ATPL; English: ICAO Level 4 or above; Minimum flight hours: F/O: 500h+, Captain: 4,000h+ (guideline); Type rating: A320 family (company may cover training costs in some cases). Under the easyJet GenX program, some courses have the company cover the type-rating cost (conditions apply).

How does easyJet pay compare to ANA or JAL?

easyJet captains average ¥27M, versus roughly ¥27M at ANA / JAL. Both figures are pre-tax; Japan applies ~33% income tax.

What is easyJet pilot salary per month?

Dividing the annual average by 12, easyJet captains earn about ¥2.25M a month and first officers about ¥1.25M. What actually lands each month moves with block hours flown, and where an annual bonus is paid the regular monthly figure sits lower with a spike in the bonus month.

What is the easyJet pilot pay scale?

easyJet first officers average ¥15M and captains ¥27M — a step of about ¥12M, or roughly 1.8×. The full range runs ¥12.1M to ¥36M, and where you sit inside it is set by seniority, type rating and the routes you fly. The seniority table on this page breaks the same range into career stages.

Is easyJet pilot salary before or after tax?

Every figure on this page is gross, before income tax. Captains average ¥27M and first officers ¥15M pre-tax, and income tax, social insurance and any local levies come out of that. Take-home depends on where you are resident and on your own deductions, so no single percentage applies. Airlines based in countries with no personal income tax — the UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia — leave more of the same gross figure in your hand.
