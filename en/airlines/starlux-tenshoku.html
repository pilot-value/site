Introduction

This guide walks through the career move into STARLUX Airlines (星宇航空 / SJX / JX) and its pilot selection process, together with every formality required between the offer and your first day. Written for Japanese pilots targeting a STARLUX job, it covers the application pack, the selection event, the resident visa application and the paperwork back home in Japan — all from first-hand experience.

The information here reflects the first half of 2025. The fine detail of application and submission documents may change. Always confirm STARLUX's current requirements on the official recruitment site.

Watch the Training Bond — if the type you currently fly differs from the type you will fly at STARLUX, resigning of your own accord within four years of upgrading to captain obliges you to repay training costs of up to NT$3 million. Check the terms before you apply.
Pay & Benefits

Salary, Staff Travel and Leave

Salary

Pay is built from base pay + a role allowance, plus a guarantee of 50 flight hours during training and 75 hours once you upgrade to captain. Taiwan pays 13 months of salary a year: the 13th month (base pay + role allowance + the 75-hour guarantee) is paid at year end regardless of company performance, starting from the year end that falls a full year after your upgrade. Depending on results, a base-pay-linked bonus may also be added at Lunar New Year.

How you are paid

Paid in New Taiwan dollars (NTD) into a Taiwanese bank account. Your pay grade is set by your years of service as a captain or first officer.

Tax

Taiwan levies income tax only — there is no equivalent of Japanese residence tax. There is no year-end adjustment, so every worker files a tax return each May.

Housing

For your first 30 days in Taiwan the company books the same hotel used during the selection event. You find your own rental in the meantime. No company accommodation is provided.

Contract

A three-year contract (Taiwanese pilots are on fixed-term contracts too). Health insurance and LOL (loss-of-license) cover included.

Home Visit Tickets

One return ticket every three months. Expires if unused for 12 months. Parents, spouse and children may also use it (destinations where family live only). Apply between three months and seven days before travel.

SNY (Supernumerary)

Available once you upgrade to captain. Limited to home-visit travel on STARLUX services to destinations where family live, twice a month. Apply at least 48 hours before the flight. Business class is possible, but seat availability may put you in economy or on the jump seat. You travel as crew in uniform (no epaulettes, badge or tie when seated in the cabin) and must report to the operations center. Statutory duty and rest limits relative to your flying duties must be taken into account.

Leave System

Annual leave runs 20–30 days depending on rank. Two programs exist for foreign crew, and you may also decline both and stay on the same system as local crew.

The system shared with local crew (bidding)
  • Long Term Bidding — two bidding windows a year (October and April); once a year you can take 7–12 consecutive days of annual leave
  • Monthly Bidding — bid by the 19th of the preceding month for up to six consecutive days of annual leave in the following month
  • Bidding Flight — bid once a month, by the 19th of the preceding month, for a specific flight and date you want to operate
  • Request DO after ANL used up — once your annual leave is exhausted, you may request up to three days off per month

Bidding points are granted every year according to rank. Taiwanese public holidays and the New Year period cost 10 pts/day, Christmas and Lunar New Year 15 pts/day, everything else 5 pts/day. Where bids clash, seniority decides.

① Commute Pattern (for foreign crew)

Eight consecutive days off every month (the company sets the dates; it can drop to six depending on circumstances). No bidding schemes other than Monthly Bidding are available. Outside the block you tend to fly long strings of consecutive duties, but your total days off per month are higher than under the other options.

② Home Visit Annual Leave (for foreign crew)

On top of the bidding schemes, one block of eight consecutive days a year on the dates you choose. Apply at least two months ahead. Counts as annual leave. Not available over Lunar New Year. No other bidding restrictions apply.

You can join or switch foreign-crew programs once you have completed the captain upgrade check (if you leave a program partway through, you can only rejoin each October).

Simulator assessment

Starlux Operations Center (about 15 minutes from the hotel by taxi)

Two versions of the ride
Applicants current on Airbus
Includes a LOFT (Line Oriented Flight Training) detail: depart RCTP, handle a High Eng Vib and return, then an ILS raw-data approach from FINAL, go-around and landing.
Everyone else
Maneuvers only. Details below.

The maneuvers profile

Two captains ride with you (one on the panel, one in the right seat). They brief you beforehand that this is an assessment of handling skill and that procedures will not be tested.

① RCTP 05L normal take-off
Manual control + auto thrust. IMC around 500ft. Runway heading, level at 3,000ft. Then switch to manual thrust, FD off, FPV (bird) displayed. Climb 1,000ft/m + 25° bank left turn; descend 1,000ft/m + right turn. Speed fixed at 250kt. Each step is called out to you verbally as you go.
② Reposition to take-off position → RTO
Start the roll again; engine failure between 100kt (the Boeing equivalent of 80kt) and V1 → RTO. The only actions required are closing the thrust levers and selecting reverse.
③ Take off again → V1 cut
An unannounced V1 cut (severe damage), flown to flap retraction. Manual handling throughout.
④ Single-engine 2,000ft ILS 05L dog leg
Single engine, manual control + auto thrust + FD. IMC to around 500ft. Land off the approach once visual. No go-around.
If you have never flown the Airbus, no one expects you to know the Airbus procedures and call-outs for an RTO or engine failure — concentrate on flying the aircraft and ask on the spot when you are unsure. The content of the assessment may vary from candidate to candidate.

The simulator assessment finishes in the afternoon, and Karen Hsu emails the result that evening. If you pass, you move on to the interview the next day (DAY 3).

From Pass to Offer

From the pass notification to the Offer Letter

1
Pass notification — proposed start date and fleet

You receive your medical certificate result together with the decision email. If you have passed, it includes a proposed start date and fleet. If either differs from what you want, negotiate before you accept. Nothing can be changed afterwards. If you are going to decline, this is your last chance.

2
Pre-hire notice — start date and fleet confirmed

Reply that you intend to join and a "Pre-hire notice" follows immediately, fixing your start date and fleet (an informal offer at this stage). Sign and return the notice and you move to the next step.

3
Work Permit application

STARLUX, as your employer, applies to the Taiwanese government. Issue takes about a month. Do not hand in your resignation until the work permit has been granted.

4
Offer Letter — the job is confirmed

Once the Labor Permit is issued the "Offer Letter" arrives and you are asked to confirm your intention to join for the last time. After you accept, "On-board Notices" are attached setting out the documents and formalities still to come. Some of them take a long time to obtain, so start early.

Documents to Submit Before You Join

Email these about a month before your start date

These are the documents due by the deadlines set out in the On-board Notices.

  • ◉
    Personal data and intellectual property consent (web form)
    Completed online via the link in the On-board Notices. It includes a field for a nickname, which becomes the name part of your company email address (e.g. "nicknameYourName@starlux.com"). Internally, everyone goes by their nickname.
  • ◉
    iPad serial number
    The iPad you use on the line is your own — the company does not issue one. iPad, iPad Air and iPad Pro all qualify (iPad mini does not). No requirement on storage size or Wi-Fi+Cellular. It must support the specified iOS version. You will install a lot of apps after joining, so reasonable storage is recommended. Send the serial number by email.
  • ◉
    Copy of your passport
    With at least ten months of validity remaining. If you hold a US C1/D visa, submit a copy of that as well.
  • ◉
    Copy of your ATPL
    Simply resend what you submitted with your application. Ratings and limitations are not required.
  • ◉
    Latest PC record
    If you have had a recurrent check since submitting your application, send a copy of the latest record. The same format as at application stage is fine.
  • ◉
    Criminal record certificate (Non criminal record)
    This becomes obtainable once you have an offer. It takes over a month, so start the application early. The certificate is issued sealed and marked "invalid if opened", but you open it to scan or photograph it. Bring the original with you on your first day (no apostille or consular legalisation required). The application procedure is covered in detail in the next section.
  • ◉
    Relationship certificate (Japanese family register + English translation)
    Proof of first-degree relatives, needed to establish EF (Employee Flight) eligibility. Obtain your family register (koseki tohon) and attach your own English translation (no certification required). After joining you upload it to the company intranet, so keep a copy in the cloud.
  • ◉
    Passport-size photographs (4.5×3.5cm)
    Used for the residence permit (ARC card) and health insurance card applications. They must meet the specification, including a white background (the same specification applies to the resident visa application).

Other formalities, depending on your circumstances

Health insurance (if family stay behind)

If your spouse works for a company, they move onto that employer's health insurance. If they work part-time or are a homemaker, they switch to National Health Insurance or continue your current cover voluntarily (up to two years).

Defined contribution pension (DC)

If you are enrolled through your employer and want to continue, join the National Pension first, then switch to individual iDeCo (within six months of leaving your job).

Children (if you move alone)

Change the named applicant to your spouse for the child medical certificate, child allowance and (in Tokyo) the 018 Support payment.

Property tax, vehicle tax and the like

You may need to register a tax agent to handle payments on assets you leave behind in Japan.
