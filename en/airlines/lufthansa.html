About Lufthansa

Lufthansa (Deutsche Lufthansa AG), founded 1926, is the German flag carrier and Europe's largest aviation group. Hubs at Frankfurt (FRA) and Munich (MUC). A founding Star Alliance member.

Group includes Swiss International Air Lines, Austrian Airlines, Brussels Airlines, and Eurowings. Following union (Vereinigung Cockpit) negotiations 2024–2026, a 5% pay increase took effect January 2026, pushing senior captain pay to EUR 266,500–279,500.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Lufthansa captain salary in 2026?

Lufthansa captain pay averages about ¥34.4M per year (range ¥24.9M–¥43.2M), pre-tax. Figures are reference values based on public data and industry benchmarks.

What is Lufthansa first officer salary?

Lufthansa first officer pay averages about ¥18.9M per year (range ¥12.6M–¥30.1M), pre-tax, rising with seniority — see the Career Ladder above for the year-by-year breakdown.

How does Lufthansa pay compare to ANA or JAL?

Lufthansa captains average ¥34.4M, versus roughly ¥27M at ANA / JAL. Both figures are pre-tax; Japan applies ~33% income tax.

What is Lufthansa pilot salary per month?

Dividing the annual average by 12, Lufthansa captains earn about ¥2.87M a month and first officers about ¥1.58M. What actually lands each month moves with block hours flown, and where an annual bonus is paid the regular monthly figure sits lower with a spike in the bonus month.

What is the Lufthansa pilot pay scale?

Lufthansa first officers average ¥18.9M and captains ¥34.4M — a step of about ¥15.5M, or roughly 1.8×. The full range runs ¥12.6M to ¥43.2M, and where you sit inside it is set by seniority, type rating and the routes you fly. The seniority table on this page breaks the same range into career stages.

Is Lufthansa pilot salary before or after tax?

Every figure on this page is gross, before income tax. Captains average ¥34.4M and first officers ¥18.9M pre-tax, and income tax, social insurance and any local levies come out of that. Take-home depends on where you are resident and on your own deductions, so no single percentage applies. Airlines based in countries with no personal income tax — the UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia — leave more of the same gross figure in your hand.
