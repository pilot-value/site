Frequently asked questions

How much do pilots earn in Asia?
Across the 26 Asia airlines on PILOT VALUE, captains average $135K ($37K〜$355K) and first officers average $70K ($19K〜$203K). That is about 14% below the $156K captain average across all 112 airlines we track, and 5th of the 7 regions we group them into.
Which airline in Asia pays pilots the most?
Of the 26 airlines listed, Cathay Pacific pays the most: captains average $226K ($189K〜$355K) and first officers $151K ($113K〜$203K).
Which country in Asia pays pilots the most?
Of the 12 countries in Asia that we cover, China is highest: captains at its 4 listed airlines average $188K.
What is the monthly pilot salary in Asia?
Dividing the regional average by 12 gives roughly $11K a month for captains and $5.9K for first officers. What actually lands each month moves with block hours flown, and where an annual bonus is paid the regular monthly figure sits lower with a spike in the bonus month.
Is pilot pay in Asia tax-free?
1 of the 26 airlines listed is based in a country that levies no personal income tax, so for that one the figures above are close to take-home. Against an identical gross salary in a country that taxes income, the amount you actually keep is materially higher.
How does it compare with Japanese airlines?
Captains at the 26 Japanese airlines we list average $116K. Asia at $135K is $18K higher.
