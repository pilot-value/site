About Porter Airlines

Porter Airlines is a Canadian premium LCC based at Toronto's Billy Bishop Airport. Through a sweeping fleet renewal away from its traditional E175s, the carrier has adopted the A220-300 and is expanding its network across domestic Canada, the U.S. East Coast, the Caribbean and Mexico. It differentiates itself with a distinctive service aimed at business travelers, offering complimentary snacks, beer and wine. The airline is growing rapidly and hiring is active.

Operations

Routes & Fleet

Routes

Operates from the Toronto hub to major Canadian cities (Vancouver, Calgary, Montreal and others) as well as the U.S. East Coast, Florida, the Caribbean and Mexico. The network is expanding on the back of the A220 introduction.

Fleet

Airbus A220-300 and Embraer E175. Approximately 80 aircraft, with further orders on backlog.

Training

Training & Checkrides

Type Rating Training (FAA-approved)

Type rating training at an FAA-certified ATO (Approved Training Organization), using Part 142 training centers such as CAE and FlightSafety. The sequence runs ground school → simulator → LOFT.

IOE (Initial Operating Experience)

After the type rating is issued, Initial Operating Experience is flown alongside an instructor captain (Check Airman). Typically around 25 to 50 legs.

Recurrent Checks (PC/LOE)

A Proficiency Check (PC) or Line Operational Evaluation (LOE) once or twice a year, conducted in accordance with FAA Part 121/135.

Upgrade to Captain

Seniority-based as a rule. Candidates must meet the required flight hours (normally 5,000–8,000 hours or more) and pass a check ride with a Check Airman. An R-ATP (1,500-hour) pathway is available.

Benefits

Pilot Benefits & Allowances

✈️
Staff Travel (Passes)

Pass travel for the employee and family — free or heavily discounted travel on the airline's own flights and with partner carriers.

🏥
Medical, Dental and Vision Insurance

Comprehensive medical coverage for the employee and family. Loss-of-license insurance is also common.

💰
401(k) Retirement Plan

A defined-contribution retirement plan (401(k)) with company matching. Matching of up to 5–16% is common.

📅
Paid Leave

Roughly 15–30 days per year, increasing with seniority. Flexible leave options such as flips and skips are available.

💵
Per Diem

A daily allowance for flying days (roughly $2–4 per hour). Rates differ between domestic and international operations.

🌐
International Flying Allowance

Additional allowances and accommodation expenses for crew operating international routes.

What is Porter Airlines pilot salary per month?

Dividing the annual average by 12, Porter Airlines captains earn about ¥2.25M a month and first officers about ¥1.13M. What actually lands each month moves with block hours flown, and where an annual bonus is paid the regular monthly figure sits lower with a spike in the bonus month.

What is the Porter Airlines pilot pay scale?

Porter Airlines first officers average ¥13.5M and captains ¥27M — a step of about ¥13.5M, or roughly 2.0×. The full range runs ¥10M to ¥32M, and where you sit inside it is set by seniority, type rating and the routes you fly. The seniority table on this page breaks the same range into career stages.

Is Porter Airlines pilot salary before or after tax?

Every figure on this page is gross, before income tax. Captains average ¥27M and first officers ¥13.5M pre-tax, and income tax, social insurance and any local levies come out of that. Take-home depends on where you are resident and on your own deductions, so no single percentage applies. Airlines based in countries with no personal income tax — the UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia — leave more of the same gross figure in your hand.
