About Ryanair

Ryanair is Europe's largest ultra-low-cost carrier (ULCC) by passenger numbers. Based in Dublin, it runs a vast network connecting 200+ cities across Europe and North Africa. A uniform Boeing 737 MAX/800 fleet and relentless cost control deliver its low fares.

Pilots need an EASA ATPL. Note that some are employed under 'pilot placement' or self-employed (Personal Service Company) contract arrangements rather than direct staff contracts.

Operations

Routes & Fleet

Routes

From Dublin, Stansted and 30+ bases across all of Europe (Spain, Italy, Portugal, Poland, etc.) plus North Africa (Morocco). Short-haul focused.

Fleet

Boeing 737 MAX 8-200 and 737-800. ~600 aircraft — one of the world's largest single-type fleets.

Training

Training & Checkrides

Type Rating (EASA-approved, cost note)

Uses EASA-certified ATOs. At LCCs, type-rating costs may be partly or fully self-funded depending on contract — confirm terms.

LIFUS

Line training under an instructor captain after type rating. Short-haul-heavy LCC operations let pilots build legs relatively quickly.

Recurrent Checks (OPC/LPC)

1–2 checks per year to EASA standards. LCCs meet the same safety standards as legacy carriers.

Upgrade

FO-to-captain upgrade typically requires 4,000–5,000+ hours. LCCs can offer more frequent upgrade opportunities.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Ryanair captain salary in 2026?

Ryanair captain pay averages about ¥22.4M per year (range ¥14.5M–¥31M), pre-tax. Figures are reference values based on public data and industry benchmarks.

What is Ryanair first officer salary?

Ryanair first officer pay averages about ¥10.3M per year (range ¥6.5M–¥14.6M), pre-tax, rising with seniority — see the Career Ladder above for the year-by-year breakdown.

What are the requirements to become a Ryanair pilot?

How does Ryanair pay compare to ANA or JAL?

Ryanair captains average ¥22.4M, versus roughly ¥27M at ANA / JAL. Both figures are pre-tax; Japan applies ~33% income tax.

What is Ryanair pilot salary per month?

Dividing the annual average by 12, Ryanair captains earn about ¥1.87M a month and first officers about ¥0.86M. What actually lands each month moves with block hours flown, and where an annual bonus is paid the regular monthly figure sits lower with a spike in the bonus month.

What is the Ryanair pilot pay scale?

Ryanair first officers average ¥10.3M and captains ¥22.4M — a step of about ¥12.1M, or roughly 2.2×. The full range runs ¥6.5M to ¥31M, and where you sit inside it is set by seniority, type rating and the routes you fly. The seniority table on this page breaks the same range into career stages.

Is Ryanair pilot salary before or after tax?

Every figure on this page is gross, before income tax. Captains average ¥22.4M and first officers ¥10.3M pre-tax, and income tax, social insurance and any local levies come out of that. Take-home depends on where you are resident and on your own deductions, so no single percentage applies. Airlines based in countries with no personal income tax — the UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia — leave more of the same gross figure in your hand.
