Frequently Asked Questions

What is GoJet Airlines captain salary in 2026?

We estimate GoJet Airlines captain pay at about $182K a year: the year-12 captain rate of $202.86 an hour on GoJet’s CRJ550 pay scale times 900 hours a year (the 75-hour monthly minimum guarantee × 12).

What is GoJet Airlines first officer salary?

We estimate GoJet Airlines first officer pay at about $95K a year: the year-6 rate of $105.61 an hour, the top of the first officer scale, times the same 900 hours.

What is the GoJet Airlines pilot salary per month?

We do not publish a monthly figure for GoJet Airlines. An annual figure divided by twelve is not what lands in a given month — what you are paid moves with the hours you fly.

What is the GoJet Airlines pilot pay scale?

We estimate annual pay for GoJet Airlines captains at about $182K. That is our own estimate from public information, not a figure GoJet Airlines publishes. These are gross figures, before income tax. The actual figure moves with the aircraft type, years of service and the routes you fly. We estimate annual pay for GoJet Airlines first officers at about $95K. Is the GoJet Airlines pilot salary before or after tax?

Every figure on this page is gross, before income tax. Take-home depends on where you are resident and on your own deductions, so no single percentage applies, and we do not publish one. Airlines based in countries with no personal income tax — the UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia — leave more of the same gross figure in your hand.
