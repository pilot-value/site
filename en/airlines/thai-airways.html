About Thai Airways International

Thai Airways International (THAI) is Thailand's flag carrier. Hubbing at Bangkok Suvarnabhumi Airport, it serves a wide network to Europe, Northeast Asia, and South Asia. After filing for business reorganization in 2020 and completing it in 2024, THAI is rebuilding its network with improved finances.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Thai Airways International captain salary in 2026?

Thai Airways International captain pay averages about ¥18.5M per year (range ¥15M–¥24.2M), pre-tax. Figures are reference values based on public data and industry benchmarks.

What is Thai Airways International first officer salary?

Thai Airways International first officer pay averages about ¥8.8M per year (range ¥6.6M–¥11.4M), pre-tax, rising with seniority — see the Career Ladder above for the year-by-year breakdown.

What are the requirements to become a Thai Airways International pilot?

Pilot Recruitment — Check official site for current openings.. Key requirements — License: ATPL (ICAO); English: ICAO Level 4+.

How does Thai Airways International pay compare to ANA or JAL?

Thai Airways International captains average ¥18.5M, versus roughly ¥27M at ANA / JAL. Both figures are pre-tax; Japan applies ~33% income tax.

What is Thai Airways International pilot salary per month?

Dividing the annual average by 12, Thai Airways International captains earn about ¥1.54M a month and first officers about ¥0.73M. What actually lands each month moves with block hours flown, and where an annual bonus is paid the regular monthly figure sits lower with a spike in the bonus month.

What is the Thai Airways International pilot pay scale?

Thai Airways International first officers average ¥8.8M and captains ¥18.5M — a step of about ¥9.7M, or roughly 2.1×. The full range runs ¥6.6M to ¥24.2M, and where you sit inside it is set by seniority, type rating and the routes you fly. The seniority table on this page breaks the same range into career stages.

Is Thai Airways International pilot salary before or after tax?

Every figure on this page is gross, before income tax. Captains average ¥18.5M and first officers ¥8.8M pre-tax, and income tax, social insurance and any local levies come out of that. Take-home depends on where you are resident and on your own deductions, so no single percentage applies. Airlines based in countries with no personal income tax — the UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia — leave more of the same gross figure in your hand.
