About Eagle Jet International, Inc.

Eagle Jet International is a Europe-based charter operator. For its A320 First Officer positions, it runs a distinctive hiring program in which EASA license holders who have not yet obtained the type rating — including low-hour pilots — are eligible to apply. For pilots straight out of training who want to build airline route experience, this is a valuable opportunity. Employment is on a contract basis (freelance included).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Eagle Jet International, Inc. captain salary in 2026?

What is Eagle Jet International, Inc. first officer salary?

How does Eagle Jet International, Inc. pay compare to ANA or JAL?

Eagle Jet International, Inc. captains average ¥14M, versus roughly ¥27M at ANA / JAL. Both figures are pre-tax; Japan applies ~33% income tax.

What is Eagle Jet International, Inc. pilot salary per month?

Dividing the annual average by 12, Eagle Jet International, Inc. captains earn about ¥1.17M a month and first officers about ¥0.63M. What actually lands each month moves with block hours flown, and where an annual bonus is paid the regular monthly figure sits lower with a spike in the bonus month.

What is the Eagle Jet International, Inc. pilot pay scale?

Eagle Jet International, Inc. first officers average ¥7.6M and captains ¥14M — a step of about ¥6.4M, or roughly 1.8×. The full range runs ¥5.5M to ¥19M, and where you sit inside it is set by seniority, type rating and the routes you fly. The seniority table on this page breaks the same range into career stages.

Is Eagle Jet International, Inc. pilot salary before or after tax?

Every figure on this page is gross, before income tax. Captains average ¥14M and first officers ¥7.6M pre-tax, and income tax, social insurance and any local levies come out of that. Take-home depends on where you are resident and on your own deductions, so no single percentage applies. Airlines based in countries with no personal income tax — the UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia — leave more of the same gross figure in your hand.
