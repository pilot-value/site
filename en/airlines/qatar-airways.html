About Qatar Airways

Qatar Airways, founded 1993, is the state-owned flag carrier of Qatar. Hubbing at Hamad International Airport (HIA) in Doha, it serves 160+ cities in 60+ countries as a Oneworld core member.

Qatar has no personal income tax, so no income tax is deducted from pay, and housing, education and transport allowances are paid on top. The airline does not publish what pilots are paid, however (checked 2026-09-29), so this page states no take-home figure.

Pilot pay — under review

Qatar Airways does not publish pilot pay. Its careers site, its past official vacancies and third-party postings carry no pay figure at all (checked 2026-09-29).
The average and range this page used to carry had no record of which document they came from. On top of that, the top of the range — which was supposed to exclude allowances — sat above the "real package including housing and education", so the two figures contradicted each other on the same page. Until a basis can be checked, the published figure is under review. The old values are not deleted — they remain in the change history.

Why it is under review
  • The careers site, past official vacancies and third-party postings contain no pilot pay figure at all.
  • The "real package including housing and education" had no source either, and the range that excluded those allowances topped out higher — a contradiction inside one page.
  • The monthly base, flying pay and housing allowance figures had no source either. We publish neither a monthly nor an annual figure.
  • Qatar has no personal income tax, which is certain. But no tax is not the same as high pay, and with no figure there is no take-home to state.
  • With no annual figure there is no monthly figure either. We do not divide by 12.

Training

Training & Checkrides

Type Rating (Direct Entry)

Qatar Airways Flight Training Center (QAFTC) in Doha. World-class modern facility.

Life in Doha

Doha is rapidly developing. English widely spoken. International schools and Japanese food available. Safe environment.

Captain Upgrade

Typically 3–7 years as F/O. Direct Entry Captains also actively recruited. Based on performance and vacancies.

Recurrent Checks

Proficiency checks 1–2x per year. International-standard training quality.

Hours Guarantee

75h/month guaranteed. Basic pay protected even if below guarantee.

Training Costs

Company covers type rating on joining (bond applies). Departure before term triggers repayment obligation.
